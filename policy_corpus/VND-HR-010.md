## 1. Purpose

This policy describes how Vendelligent Technologies LLC ("Vendelligent", "the Company") sets goals, gives feedback, rates performance, promotes people and supports their development. It aims to make performance expectations clear and fair, consistent with the values "Own the outcome", "Build with data" and "Respect by default".

## 2. Scope

The policy applies to all full-time (FT) and part-time (PT) employees in Riyadh HQ, the Dubai office and the Remote Engineering Hub. Contractors (CT) and interns (IN) receive goals and feedback from their supervisor but are not part of the annual rating, calibration or promotion processes.

## 3. Definitions

- **OKR:** Objectives and Key Results, the quarterly goal framework.
- **1:1:** a scheduled one-to-one conversation between an employee and their line manager.
- **Calibration:** a review by Directors to ensure ratings are applied consistently across teams.
- **PIP:** Performance Improvement Plan.
- **Probation:** the first 90 calendar days of employment for FT and PT employees, extendable once by up to 90 days (maximum 180 days in total).
- **VendHub:** the Company people portal where goals, reviews and ratings are recorded.
- **Grades:** L1 Associate, L2 Professional, L3 Senior, L4 Lead/Manager, L5 Director, L6 Executive.

## 4. Quarterly OKRs

- Each employee agrees OKRs with their line manager within the first 10 working days of each quarter, and records them in VendHub.
- Company OKRs are set by the CEO and leadership team; department and individual OKRs must align to them.
- Each employee should have between 2 and 4 objectives per quarter, each with measurable key results.
- OKRs are reviewed at mid-quarter and closed out in the final two weeks of the quarter, with a self-assessment and manager comment in VendHub.

## 5. Continuous Feedback and 1:1s

Line managers must hold a 1:1 with each direct report **at least once every two weeks (fortnightly)**. 1:1s cover progress against OKRs, blockers, wellbeing, recognition and development. Managers give timely feedback close to the event rather than saving it for the annual review. Employees are encouraged to request feedback from peers and cross-functional partners, including brand-partner-facing and Smart Machine field teams where relevant. Brief notes of agreed actions are kept in VendHub.

## 6. Annual Performance Review

### 6.1 Timing

The annual performance review takes place in **January and February** and covers the previous calendar year (the Company fiscal year runs 1 January to 31 December).

### 6.2 Process

1. The employee completes a self-assessment in VendHub.
2. The line manager writes an assessment using OKR results, feedback received and the Company values, and proposes a rating.
3. Directors calibrate proposed ratings across their departments.
4. The line manager shares the final rating and feedback in a review meeting, and the employee may add a written comment in VendHub.

### 6.3 Rating scale

| Rating | Label | Descriptor |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Below | Did not meet the core expectations of the role; significant improvement needed |
| 2 | Partially Meets | Met some expectations but with notable gaps in results or behaviours |
| 3 | Meets | Fully met the expectations of the role and OKRs; reliable contributor |
| 4 | Exceeds | Consistently went beyond expectations; delivered strong impact |
| 5 | Exceptional | Outstanding, rare impact on the Company, its customers or its people; role model for the values |

### 6.4 Calibration

Directors meet in February to review proposed ratings. Their purpose is consistency between teams, not to force a distribution. The Head of People & Culture facilitates and records any rating changes and the reasons. A manager must tell the employee if a rating differs from the manager's original proposal as a result of calibration.

## 7. Link to Salary Review and Promotion

- The final annual rating is a key input to the **annual salary review**, which takes place in Q1 with increases effective **1 April** (see VND-HR-009).
- Promotions are considered in two cycles, **April** and **October**. Managers nominate employees through VendHub; Directors review nominations; the Head of People & Culture and CEO approve promotions to L4 and above.
- A rating of 1 or 2 normally means no salary increase and no promotion in that cycle. A rating of 3 is eligible for a market-based adjustment. Ratings of 4 and 5 are eligible for merit increases and promotion consideration.
- A current PIP or open final written warning (VND-HR-011) makes an employee ineligible for promotion until it is closed.

## 8. Promotion Criteria and Career Ladders

### 8.1 Promotion criteria by grade

| Promotion to | Core expectation | Typical evidence |
|---|---|---|
| L2 Professional | Works independently on defined tasks | Delivers OKRs with limited guidance; rating of 3 or higher |
| L3 Senior | Owns significant outcomes and guides others | Led a project end to end; mentored at least one colleague; rating of 4 sustained over 2 reviews |
| L4 Lead/Manager | Leads people or a major technical area | Demonstrated team or technical leadership; cross-team influence; Director sponsorship |
| L5 Director | Leads a function or department | Business-level results; strategic scope; CEO approval |
| L6 Executive | Company-wide accountability | Appointed by CEO and Board |

An employee must normally have held their current grade for at least 12 months and completed probation to be nominated.

### 8.2 Career ladders

Vendelligent offers two ladders:

- **Individual contributor (IC) ladder:** L1 to L4 and above for deep experts (for example senior engineers, data scientists, field technicians). IC roles at L4 carry the same grade standing as managers, including ESOP eligibility at hire (see VND-HR-009).
- **Management ladder:** L4 Lead/Manager and L5 Director, for those who lead people and teams.

Moving between ladders is possible at L3 or L4 by agreement with the Director and People & Culture.

## 9. Probation Review and 30/60/90 Check-ins

### 9.1 Check-ins

New FT and PT employees have structured check-ins with their line manager at **30, 60 and 90 days** (see VND-SOP-101). A buddy is assigned for the first 60 days.

### 9.2 Probation review at day 80

The line manager completes a probation review in VendHub at **day 80** and discusses it with the employee. The outcome is one of:

| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| Confirm | Employment is confirmed at day 90; benefits requiring probation completion begin (VND-HR-009) |
| Extend | Probation is extended once, by up to 90 days (maximum 180 days in total), with written targets and a review date |
| Exit | Employment ends during probation with 1 week of notice |

The decision is approved by the Director and People & Culture before it is communicated. Exit during probation is not a disciplinary action.

## 10. Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)

### 10.1 When a PIP is used

A PIP is used when an employee's performance is below expectations despite feedback and support, for example a rating of 1 or a sustained pattern of missed OKRs. Misconduct is dealt with under VND-HR-011, not through a PIP.

### 10.2 Structure

- Duration: **60 days**.
- The manager and a People & Culture partner meet the employee, explain the concerns, and agree specific, measurable targets and support in writing.
- **Weekly check-ins** between manager and employee. Each is documented in VendHub.
- The employee may be accompanied by a colleague at the opening and closing PIP meetings.

### 10.3 Outcomes

| Outcome | Result |
|---|---|
| Successful | Performance meets expectations; PIP closed; normal reviews resume |
| Extended | Extended once, by up to 30 days, where there is clear progress or a documented external cause such as medical leave |
| Unsuccessful | Moves to the disciplinary process for capability, which may lead to dismissal under VND-HR-011 |

## 11. Learning and Development, Study Leave and Mentorship

- **L&D budget:** full-time employees who have completed probation have SAR 3,000 per calendar year for books, courses, conferences and certifications, claimed via the L&D form in VendHub with manager approval. Unused budget does not roll over (VND-HR-009). Managers should link L&D choices to the employee's development plan.
- **Study and exam leave:** up to 5 working days per year for approved certifications or degree exams (VND-HR-004).
- **Mentorship:** employees may request a mentor outside their reporting line through PeopleDesk (category "Other"). The buddy assigned at onboarding covers the first 60 days only.
- **Interns and contractors** are not eligible for the L&D budget but receive coaching from their supervisor.

## 12. Rating Records, Visibility and Appeals

### 12.1 Storage and access

Final ratings, review comments, PIP records and probation reviews are stored in VendHub. They are visible to the employee, the employee's line manager and management chain, the Head of People & Culture and the People & Culture partners who support the process. They are Restricted employee records under VND-HR-008 and must not be shared outside this group, or posted in Slack or email.

### 12.2 Appeals

An employee who disagrees with their annual rating may appeal in writing to the **Head of People & Culture within 10 working days** of receiving the rating, stating the grounds (for example factual error, procedural unfairness or bias). The Head of People & Culture reviews the evidence, may consult the Director and decides within 10 working days. Where the Head of People & Culture is involved, the CEO appoints another reviewer. The decision is final.

## 13. Frequently Asked Questions

**How often must my manager meet me?** At least once every two weeks for a 1:1.

**When is my annual review?** In January and February, covering the previous calendar year.

**What happens at day 80 of probation?** Your manager completes a probation review and one of three outcomes follows: confirm, extend once by up to 90 days, or exit with 1 week's notice.

**How long is a PIP?** 60 days with weekly documented check-ins, and a possible one-time extension of up to 30 days.

**Who sees my rating?** You, your management chain, the Head of People & Culture and supporting People & Culture partners.

**I disagree with my rating. What can I do?** Appeal in writing to the Head of People & Culture within 10 working days.

## 14. Roles and Responsibilities

- **Employees:** set and update OKRs, request feedback, complete self-assessments and engage with development plans.
- **Line managers:** hold fortnightly 1:1s, document feedback, rate fairly with evidence, and run probation reviews and PIPs on time.
- **Directors:** calibrate ratings, nominate and review promotions, and sponsor development.
- **People & Culture:** run the review calendar, facilitate calibration, support PIPs and handle appeals.
- **Head of People & Culture:** owns this policy and decides rating appeals.

## 15. Exceptions and Escalation

Exceptions to timelines in this policy (for example, a delay caused by extended leave) require the Head of People & Culture's written approval. Concerns about unfair treatment in performance matters may be raised through PeopleDesk or as a grievance under VND-HR-002.
