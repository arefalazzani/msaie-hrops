## 1. Purpose

This SOP defines how Vendelligent Technologies LLC ("Vendelligent", "the Company") manages an employee's departure, whether by resignation, termination by the Company or end of a fixed-term contract. It protects continuity of work on Smart Machines and brand-partner commitments, secures Company assets and consumer data, and ensures the departing employee receives their final settlement correctly and on time.

## 2. Scope

This SOP covers all employment types: full-time (FT), part-time (PT), fixed-term/contractor (CT) and intern (IN). Sections on notice periods and gratuity apply to FT and PT employees. Contractors and interns follow the end-date in their agreement and are not eligible for gratuity.

## 3. Definitions

| Final settlement | The last payment through VendPay: salary to the last day, leave payout, gratuity and any deductions. |

## 5. Procedure

### 5.1 Departure types and notice periods

| Departure type | Trigger | Notice / end |
|---|---|---|
| Resignation during probation | Written notice via the VendHub "Resignation" form | 1 week |
| Resignation after probation, grades L1 to L3 | VendHub "Resignation" form | 30 days |
| Resignation after probation, grades L4 and above | VendHub "Resignation" form | 60 days |
| Termination by the Company | Outcome of a process under VND-HR-011 Disciplinary Policy and Procedure, or redundancy | As stated in the termination letter and applicable law |
| End of fixed-term contract | Contract end date | No notice; reminder from P&C 30 days before end |

Resignation must be submitted in writing through the VendHub "Resignation" form; email or Slack resignations are not valid. The Company may place an employee on garden leave for all or part of the notice period at its discretion; salary and benefits continue during garden leave.

### 5.2 Step-by-step procedure

| Step | Who | Action | System | Timing / SLA |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Employee | Submits the "Resignation" form stating the proposed last day. | VendHub | Day of notice |
| 2 | P&C | Acknowledges the resignation, confirms the last day and notice period, and opens an Offboarding case. | PeopleDesk | Within 2 working days |
| 3 | Line manager | Meets the employee, confirms the handover scope and decides on garden leave with P&C. | VendHub | Within 3 working days of acknowledgement |
| 4 | Employee | Drafts the KT document; manager reviews and approves. | Google Workspace | Draft by the end of week 1 of notice (or 2 working days for 1-week notice) |
| 5 | Line manager | Agrees the handover plan with named successors and dates. | VendHub | Within 5 working days of acknowledgement |
| 6 | P&C | Schedules the exit interview with the employee. | PeopleDesk | Last week of employment |
| 7 | P&C | Confirms benefits end dates and visa cancellation steps (section 5.5). | VendHub | At least 10 working days before the last day where notice allows |
| 8 | Employee | Raises a return ticket and returns laptop, charger, headset, badge and any other tagged `VT-xxxxx` assets. | SecureVend | Within 5 working days of the last day |
| 9 | IT | Revokes access: Okta, Slack, Google Workspace, GitHub, VPN, VendHub. | SecureVend, Okta | End of the last day |
| 10 | Finance, P&C | Calculates final settlement: salary, leave payout, gratuity, deductions. | VendPay | Within 14 days of the last day |
| 11 | P&C | Issues the experience certificate on request. | PeopleDesk | Within 5 working days of request |

For termination by the Company (see VND-HR-011), steps 4 to 12 apply with timelines set by the termination letter. Access may be revoked immediately at the decision of the Head of People & Culture and the CTO or COO where there is a security risk.

### 5.3 Final settlement components

| Component | Rule |
|---|---|
| Salary | Paid to the last day on a daily basis. |
| Leave payout | Accrued, unused annual leave is paid out at final settlement. Annual leave accrues at approximately 1.83 days/month, or 2.5 days/month after 5 completed years. Leave taken beyond accrual is deducted. |
| Gratuity | Calculated per VND-HR-009 Compensation, Payroll and Benefits Policy: half a month's basic salary per year for years 1 to 5 and one month per year thereafter, pro-rata, for FT and PT only. Resignation before 2 years: no gratuity; 2 to 5 years: one-third; 5 to 10 years: two-thirds; over 10 years: full. |
| Deductions | Unreturned assets at depreciated value; outstanding advances; unpaid loans. |
| Timing | Within 14 days of the last day via VendPay. |

### 5.4 Asset return and access

- Assets must be returned within 5 working days of the last day. Raise a return ticket in SecureVend; IT confirms receipt against the asset tag.
- Assets not returned are deducted from the final settlement at depreciated value, in line with VND-HR-007.
- Access is revoked at the end of the last day. During garden leave, access to Restricted data (consumer PII from Smart Machines, brand-partner pricing, employee records) is removed from the start of garden leave.
- Personal files must be removed from Company devices before return; the Company does not recover personal data.

### 5.5 Benefits, visa cancellation and certificates

- **Medical insurance** ends on the last day of employment. The Employee Assistance Programme (EAP) remains available for 30 days after the last day.
- **Visa cancellation (synthetic timelines):** for KSA and UAE sponsored employees, P&C starts residence-permit cancellation on the last day; the cancellation is completed within about 10 working days. The employee should plan to leave the country or transfer sponsorship within the grace period set by the authority (typically 30 to 60 days).
- **Experience certificate:** issued on request through PeopleDesk (category Letter Request) within 5 working days.
- **Life and disability cover** for FT employees ends on the last day.

## 7. Failure and Exception Handling

| Situation | Action |
|---|---|
| Employee leaves without serving notice | P&C records it as a notice breach; the shortfall may be deducted from final settlement to the extent permitted by law. |
| Employee does not return assets | IT escalates after 5 working days; the depreciated value is deducted at final settlement. |
| KT document is incomplete | The manager extends handover by agreement or assigns an interim owner and informs P&C. |
| Final settlement late | P&C raises a P2 PeopleDesk ticket and notifies the COO; payment is expedited. |
| Security risk on exit | Access is revoked immediately and a SecureVend security ticket is opened. |
| Employee on sick or other leave during notice | The notice period is not extended unless agreed in writing. |

## 8. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1. How do I resign?** Submit the "Resignation" form in VendHub. Email and Slack resignations are not valid.

**Q2. How long is my notice period?** During probation 1 week. After probation 30 days for L1 to L3 and 60 days for L4 and above.

**Q3. When will I be paid my final settlement?** Within 14 days of your last day, including your accrued unused leave payout.

**Q4. Do I keep medical cover after leaving?** Medical insurance ends on your last day. The EAP is available for 30 days after.

**Q5. What if I do not return my laptop?** You have 5 working days after your last day. After that, the depreciated value is deducted from your final settlement.

**Q6. Can I take leave during my notice period?** Leave still requires approval under VND-SOP-103, and the manager may decline leave that jeopardises handover.

## 9. Roles and Responsibilities

- **Employee:** submits the resignation, completes the KT document, returns assets and attends the exit interview.
- **Line manager:** approves the handover, decides garden leave with P&C and reassigns work.
- **People & Culture:** acknowledges, runs the exit interview, manages benefits, visa and certificates, and records rehire eligibility.
- **IT (SecureVend):** processes returns and revokes access.
- **Finance:** prepares and pays the final settlement through VendPay.

## 10. Exceptions and Escalation

Requests to shorten or waive a notice period, or to change settlement timing, are raised in PeopleDesk and approved by the Head of People & Culture. Disputes about settlement are escalated to the COO. Grievances raised during exit follow VND-HR-002.
