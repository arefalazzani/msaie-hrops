## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out how Vendelligent Technologies LLC ("Vendelligent", "the Company") responds to misconduct and to serious or repeated failures to meet required standards. Its aim is to correct behaviour fairly where possible, protect colleagues, consumers, brand partners and the Company, and make sure any action is consistent, proportionate and properly documented.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees in Riyadh HQ, the Dubai office, the Remote Engineering Hub and field locations where Smart Machines are installed and maintained. It covers conduct as set out in VND-HR-001 and other Company policies. Poor performance that is not misconduct is handled under VND-HR-010 (Performance Improvement Plan). This policy does not replace the grievance process (VND-HR-002) or limit rights under applicable labour law.

## 3. Definitions

- **Misconduct:** a breach of Company policy or standards that is serious enough to warrant formal action but does not by itself destroy trust in the employee.
- **Gross misconduct:** conduct so serious that it may justify dismissal without prior warnings.
- **Investigating manager:** a manager not involved in the incident, appointed to establish the facts.
- **People & Culture partner:** the HR professional who advises on process and ensures fairness.
- **Hearing:** a formal meeting at which the employee responds to the allegations.
- **Working day:** a day on which the employee's Anchor Office is open.

## 4. Principles

1. **Fairness:** allegations are investigated without pre-judgement and decisions rest on evidence.
2. **Proportionality:** the action taken matches the seriousness of the conduct and the employee's record.
3. **Right to be heard:** the employee is told what is alleged and given a genuine opportunity to respond before any decision.
4. **Consistency:** similar cases are treated similarly across teams and grades.
5. **Confidentiality:** information is shared only with those who need it, in line with VND-HR-008.
6. **Respect by default:** the process must be carried out courteously and without retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith, including through SpeakUp.

## 5. Informal Stage

For minor issues such as occasional lateness or a first lapse in workplace etiquette, the line manager should first talk to the employee, explain the standard expected and agree what will change. The manager records a brief note in VendHub. An informal conversation is not a disciplinary sanction. If the behaviour continues or the matter is more serious, the manager consults People & Culture and the formal procedure begins.

## 6. Formal Stages

| Stage | Sanction | Validity | When used |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Verbal warning | Recorded; remains active for 3 months | Minor misconduct or repeated informal issues |
| 2 | First written warning | Valid for **6 months** | More serious misconduct, or further misconduct while a verbal warning is active |
| 3 | Final written warning | Valid for **12 months** | Serious misconduct, or further misconduct while a first written warning is active |
| 4 | Dismissal | Employment ends, with notice or payment as the law requires | Further misconduct while a final written warning is active, or gross misconduct |

Stages may be skipped where the conduct warrants it. Gross misconduct may lead directly to dismissal. The sanction is decided by a manager at least one level above the investigating manager, with advice from People & Culture. Dismissal must be approved by the Director and the Head of People & Culture (and by the CEO for L4 and above).

## 7. Examples of Misconduct and Gross Misconduct

### 7.1 Misconduct (examples)

- Repeated lateness or unreported absence (see also section 12).
- Failure to follow reasonable manager instructions.
- Minor breaches of the equipment or expense procedures.
- Disrespectful language or behaviour that does not amount to harassment.
- Failure to complete mandatory training by the deadline.
- Minor security lapses, such as not locking a screen.

### 7.2 Gross misconduct (examples)

The Company has zero tolerance for the following:

- **Falsifying expenses** or other records, or claiming for items not incurred.
- **Unauthorised disclosure of Restricted data**, including consumer data from Smart Machines, payment data, brand-partner pricing, model weights or training data, and employee records.
- **Harassment, discrimination or retaliation** (see VND-HR-002).
- **Violence**, threats or deliberately endangering safety, including breaches of site safety rules in retail locations.
- **Working under the influence** of alcohol or illegal drugs.
- **Bribery**, corruption or accepting improper inducements from brand partners or suppliers.
- **A serious breach of VND-HR-008** (Information Security, Data Protection and AI Acceptable Use), for example putting Restricted data into a public AI tool or failing to report a suspected breach within 1 hour.
- **A serious breach of VND-HR-005** (Remote, Hybrid and Work-from-Anywhere), for example working from a Restricted country or accessing Smart Machine production data from a restricted location.
- Theft, fraud or deliberate damage to Company property.

This list is illustrative, not exhaustive.

## 8. Investigation Procedure

| Step | Who | Action | Timing |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Line manager | Reports the concern to People & Culture through PeopleDesk | Immediately |
| 2 | People & Culture | Decides whether a formal investigation is needed and appoints an investigating manager and a People & Culture partner | Within 2 working days |
| 3 | Investigating manager | Gathers evidence, interviews witnesses and the employee, keeps notes | Complete within **10 working days** of appointment |
| 4 | Investigating manager | Submits a written report with findings and a recommendation | At the end of the investigation |
| 5 | People & Culture | Sends the employee a written hearing invitation with the allegations and evidence | At least **3 working days'** notice before the hearing |
| 6 | Decision maker | Holds the hearing, considers the employee's response and decides | At or shortly after the hearing |
| 7 | Decision maker | Gives the outcome in writing, with reasons and appeal rights | Within **5 working days** of the hearing |

Where an investigation cannot be completed within 10 working days, the Head of People & Culture may extend it and the employee must be told the reason and the new date. Investigators must not be in the employee's reporting line or have a personal interest in the outcome. Allegations of harassment or discrimination are P1 cases handled under VND-SOP-104 and VND-HR-002 and are restricted to People & Culture.

## 9. Suspension

Where the allegation is serious, or presence at work could put people, evidence or data at risk, the Company may suspend the employee **on full pay**. Suspension is a neutral act, not a sanction, and is not an assumption of guilt.

- Suspension is decided by the Director with the Head of People & Culture and confirmed in writing.
- It must not last more than **10 working days** initially. It may be extended in writing by the Head of People & Culture where the investigation requires, with the reason and new end date stated.
- During suspension the employee's access is suspended in SecureVend, but benefits continue.
- The employee remains available to take part in the investigation.

## 10. Hearing and Rights

- The employee has the right to be **accompanied by a colleague** (an employee of Vendelligent not involved in the matter) at a hearing.
- The employee will receive the allegations and supporting evidence in advance, may present their own evidence and may call relevant witnesses.
- The employee should tell People & Culture in advance if a reasonable adjustment or interpreter is needed.
- If the employee or their companion cannot attend, the hearing may be rearranged once within 3 working days. If the employee fails to attend a second time without good reason, the decision may be made on the evidence available.
- Notes of the hearing are kept by a People & Culture partner.

## 11. Appeals, Records and Retention

### 11.1 Appeal

An employee may appeal a warning or dismissal in writing to the Head of People & Culture **within 5 working days** of receiving the written outcome, stating the grounds. The appeal is heard by a **Director not previously involved** in the case. The Director may uphold, reduce or overturn the sanction but cannot increase it. The appeal decision is given in writing within 5 working days of the appeal hearing and is final.

### 11.2 Record keeping

- Warnings are recorded in the employee's confidential file in VendHub.
- A warning **expires** after its validity period (6 months for a first written warning; 12 months for a final written warning) and is then disregarded for deciding future sanctions, but **stays on file**.
- Disciplinary records are visible only to **People & Culture**. Managers receive only the information needed to apply the outcome.
- Records of unfounded allegations are not kept in the employee's file.

## 12. Relationship to Other Processes

- **Attendance (VND-HR-003):** three unreported absences within a rolling 30 days lead to an attendance review. The review is a conversation. If it finds unjustified absence, it may lead to the formal stages in section 6.
- **Performance (VND-HR-010):** poor performance without misconduct follows the 60-day PIP. An unsuccessful PIP may lead to a capability hearing under this procedure.
- **Grievances (VND-HR-002):** a grievance raised during a disciplinary process is handled separately, and the disciplinary process may be paused if necessary.

## 13. Managers' Responsibilities and Consequences

Managers must follow this procedure and must not impose warnings, suspensions or dismissals on their own authority. A manager who skips required steps, delays without reason, breaches confidentiality, or retaliates against a complainant or witness may be subject to disciplinary action. A sanction imposed without following the procedure may be set aside on appeal.

## 14. Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I be dismissed without a warning?** Yes, for gross misconduct such as falsifying expenses or disclosing Restricted data without authority.

**How much notice will I get of a disciplinary hearing?** At least 3 working days, with the allegations and evidence.

**Can I bring someone with me?** Yes, a colleague who is not involved in the case.

**How long does a first written warning last?** 6 months. A final written warning lasts 12 months. After expiry the warning stays on file but is disregarded for later sanctions.

**How long can I be suspended?** Up to 10 working days on full pay, extendable in writing by the Head of People & Culture.

**How do I appeal?** In writing to the Head of People & Culture within 5 working days; a Director not previously involved decides.

## 15. Roles and Responsibilities

- **Employees:** comply with Company policies, cooperate with investigations and keep matters confidential.
- **Line managers:** raise concerns promptly, use the informal stage appropriately and implement outcomes.
- **Investigating managers:** investigate impartially within 10 working days.
- **People & Culture:** advise on process, keep records and ensure fairness and timeliness.
- **Directors:** decide sanctions, approve suspensions and hear appeals when uninvolved.
- **Head of People & Culture:** owns this policy and approves extensions.

## 16. Exceptions and Escalation

Deviations from the timelines or stages in this policy require written approval from the Head of People & Culture. Where a Director or the Head of People & Culture is the subject of an allegation, the matter is escalated to the CEO (or the COO if the CEO is involved). Employees may also use the anonymous SpeakUp channel to report concerns.
