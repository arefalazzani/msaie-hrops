## 1. Purpose

This Employee Handbook introduces Vendelligent Technologies LLC ("Vendelligent" or "the Company"), explains what the Company does and what it values, defines employment types and grades, and describes how the HR policy suite is organised and how employees can get answers. It is the entry point to every other document in the VND-HR and VND-SOP series. Where this Handbook summarises a rule, the detailed policy named in the summary is the authoritative source.

## 2. Scope

This Handbook applies to every person working for Vendelligent in any of the four employment types: Full-time (FT), Part-time (PT), Fixed-term / Contractor (CT) and Intern (IN). It applies in Riyadh, Dubai, at retail partner sites where Smart Machines are installed, and to employees working remotely. Individual benefits depend on employment type, grade and probation status, as set out in section 6.

## 3. Definitions

- **Smart Machine**: an intelligent vending or kiosk machine installed in a retail location, with a human-like AI assistant that replaces a traditional sales interaction.
- **Marketplace**: the Company's AI-driven online marketplace that personalises product discovery for consumers.
- **Brand partner**: a perfume, coffee or other product brand whose products are sold through Smart Machines or the Marketplace.
- **Retail partner**: the owner or operator of a retail location (for example a mall or shop) that hosts Smart Machines.
- **Anchor Office**: the office (Riyadh or Dubai) whose working week, public holidays and core-hours clock apply to an employee. It is shown in the employee's VendHub profile.
- **Policy**: a document that sets rules and entitlements (VND-HR-0xx). **SOP**: a Standard Operating Procedure that sets the steps for carrying out a process (VND-SOP-1xx).
- **Working day**: a day on which the employee's Anchor Office is open (Sunday–Thursday for Riyadh, Monday–Friday for Dubai), excluding public holidays.

## 4. About Vendelligent

### 4.1 Mission

Vendelligent's mission is to transform the consumer experience in physical retail with artificial intelligence. The Company combines Smart Machines in retail locations with an AI-driven marketplace that personalises product discovery for each consumer.

### 4.2 Products and launch categories

The Company launched with two categories, **perfumes** and **coffee**. Smart Machines guide consumers through a conversation rather than a shelf, recommending products based on preferences. The Marketplace extends the same personalisation online. The Company works with retail partners that host machines and brand partners whose products are offered. ## 7. The Policy Framework

### 7.1 Policies and SOPs

A **policy** states the rule: what is allowed, required or prohibited, and what an employee is entitled to. An **SOP** states the procedure: who does what, in which system and by when. Policies are approved by the CEO. SOPs are approved by the COO. Both are owned by the Head of People & Culture.

### 7.2 Document IDs

Policies use the format `VND-HR-0xx` (for example VND-HR-004 Leave Policy). SOPs use `VND-SOP-1xx` (for example VND-SOP-103 PTO Request and Approval SOP). Every document shows its version, effective date, next review date, owner, approver and classification. The current suite is version 2.1, effective 1 January 2026, next review 1 January 2027.

### 7.3 Where to find documents

All policies and SOPs are in the **policy library in VendHub**, the Company's people portal. Employees sign in through Okta SSO with MFA. If two documents appear to conflict, the more specific policy prevails and the employee should raise a Policy Question in PeopleDesk.

### 7.4 Asking questions

1. **AI HR Assistant (Tier-0).** The first stop for policy questions. It can answer from published policies and prepare draft tickets or emails. It **cannot** approve leave, change records or close cases. Sensitive matters, namely harassment, discrimination, medical details and legal threats, are always routed to a human PeopleDesk ticket (P1 or P2).
2. **PeopleDesk.** The HR ticketing system (ticket IDs such as PD-2026-00123). Categories include Leave & Attendance, Payroll & Compensation, Benefits, Remote Work & WFA, Expenses, Grievance, Harassment/Discrimination, Policy Question and Letter Request. Equipment issues are redirected to SecureVend.
3. **Line manager** for day-to-day questions.

Target response times (first response / resolution): P1 safety or harassment allegation, 4 business hours / investigation started within 2 working days; P2 payroll error or visa or legal deadline, 1 working day / 5 working days; P3 benefits, letters and WFA, 2 working days / 7 working days; P4 general policy question, 2 working days / 10 working days. See VND-SOP-104.

### 8.1 Frequently asked questions

**Where do I find a policy?** In the VendHub policy library. Ask the AI HR Assistant if you are unsure which document applies.

**Can the AI HR Assistant approve my leave?** No. Leave is requested and approved only in VendHub. The assistant can explain the rules and prepare a draft ticket.

**I am a contractor. Do I get annual leave or a learning budget?** No. Contractors (CT) do not accrue leave and are not eligible for the learning budget, medical insurance, home-office stipend or gratuity.

**Whose public holidays do I follow if I work remotely?** Those of your Anchor Office, as shown in VendHub.

**How long is probation, and can it be extended?** 90 calendar days for FT and PT. It can be extended once by up to 90 days, with a maximum of 180 days.

**Who do I contact about a harassment concern?** Your manager, a PeopleDesk ticket in the "Harassment/Discrimination" category, or SpeakUp. See VND-HR-002.

## 9. Roles and Responsibilities

- **Employees** read this Handbook and the policies relevant to their role, complete mandatory training and ask when unsure.
- **Line managers** apply policies consistently, approve requests within the stated timeframes and escalate concerns.
- **Head of People & Culture** owns the policy suite, reviews it annually and publishes it in VendHub.
- **CEO** approves policies. **COO** approves SOPs.
- **Legal & Compliance** advises on legal changes that affect policies.

## 10. Exceptions and Escalation

Exceptions to any HR policy must be requested through a PeopleDesk ticket and approved in writing by the Head of People & Culture, and by the CEO where the policy requires it. Exceptions are recorded in the ticket. Questions that cannot be answered by the AI HR Assistant or a manager are escalated to People & Culture through PeopleDesk.
