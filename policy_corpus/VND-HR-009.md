## 1. Purpose

This policy explains how Vendelligent Technologies LLC ("Vendelligent", "the Company") pays and rewards its people. It sets out the pay philosophy, the grade structure, salary components, the monthly payroll process, deductions and advances, how payroll errors are corrected, the annual salary review and promotion cycles, and every employee benefit together with who is eligible for it.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to full-time (FT), part-time (PT), fixed-term/contractor (CT) and intern (IN) personnel at all Company locations. Eligibility differs by employment type; section 17 contains a single benefits eligibility matrix. Where an individual contract is more favourable than this policy on a point required by applicable labour law, the contract and the law prevail.

## 3. Definitions

- **Basic salary:** the fixed monthly base pay stated in the employment contract, excluding allowances.
- **Probation:** the first 90 calendar days of employment for FT and PT employees, extendable once by up to 90 days (maximum 180 days in total).
- **VendHub:** the Company people portal (HRIS), where payslips, forms and performance records are held.
- **VendPay:** the payroll module inside VendHub.
- **PeopleDesk:** the HR ticketing system (ticket IDs such as PD-2026-00123).
- **WPS:** the Wage Protection System; payroll is paid by WPS-compliant bank transfer.
- **Grade:** the level L1 to L6 assigned to a role.
- **Working day:** a day on which the employee's Anchor Office is open (Sunday to Thursday for Riyadh, Monday to Friday for Dubai).

## 4. Pay Philosophy and Grade Structure

### 4.1 Pay philosophy

Vendelligent pays for the outcomes an employee owns, in line with the value "Own the outcome". Pay is set to be fair internally (the same grade and responsibility are paid within the same band) and competitive externally for Riyadh, Dubai and remote technology talent. Pay decisions are based on role, grade, performance and market data, and never on gender, nationality, religion, age, disability or any other protected characteristic (see VND-HR-002).

### 4.2 Grade structure

| Grade | Title | Typical scope |
|---|---|---|
| L1 | Associate | Entry level; works under close guidance |
| L2 | Professional | Independent contributor on defined work |
| L3 | Senior | Owns significant outcomes; guides others |
| L4 | Lead / Manager | Leads a team or a major technical area |
| L5 | Director | Leads a function or department |
| L6 | Executive (C-level) | Company-wide accountability |

### 4.3 Salary bands

Each grade has a salary band. Bands are confidential and held by People & Culture. They are not published in this policy or in VendHub. An employee may ask People & Culture through PeopleDesk (category "Payroll & Compensation") which grade they hold and where their basic salary sits relative to the band midpoint.

## 5. Salary Components

### 5.1 Components

| Component | Rule |
|---|---|
| Basic salary | Stated in the employment contract; reviewed annually (section 9) |
| Housing allowance | 25% of basic salary, paid monthly |
| Transport allowance | SAR 500 per month, paid monthly |
| Overtime (non-exempt roles) | 150% of hourly rate for pre-approved overtime; 200% or time off in lieu (TOIL), at the employee's choice, for weekend or public holiday work (see VND-HR-003) |
| Mobile allowance | SAR 150 per month for designated customer/field-facing roles (section 15.3) |

Exempt roles (engineers, managers and professional staff) do not receive overtime pay. Allowance amounts in this policy are synthetic and apply equally to all grades.

### 5.2 Pro-rata for part-time

Part-time employees (20 to 32 hours per week) receive basic salary and allowances pro-rata to their contracted hours.

### 5.3 Interns and contractors

Interns receive a monthly stipend stated in the internship agreement. Contractors are paid the fee stated in the contractor agreement against approved invoices and do not receive allowances, benefits or gratuity unless the agreement says so.

## 6. Payroll

### 6.1 Pay date

Salaries are paid monthly on the **25th** of each month, or on the preceding working day when the 25th is not a working day. Payment is made by WPS-compliant bank transfer to the account registered in VendHub.

### 6.2 Cut-off

Changes to bank details, allowances, overtime and approved expense reimbursements must be completed in VendHub before the payroll cut-off. Expense claims approved by the 15th are reimbursed in that month's payroll (see VND-HR-006).

### 6.3 Payslips

Payslips are published in VendHub on or before the pay date. Employees are responsible for checking each payslip and reporting any discrepancy promptly (section 8).

## 7. Deductions and Advances

### 7.1 Permitted deductions

Deductions from pay are made only where permitted by law or agreed in writing, for example statutory social insurance contributions, approved salary advance repayments, and unreturned Company assets deducted at depreciated value from the final settlement (see VND-HR-007).

### 7.2 Salary advance

- A full-time or part-time employee may request a salary advance of up to **50% of one month's basic salary**.
- Only **one advance per calendar year** is permitted.
- The advance is repaid in equal deductions over **3 months** starting with the next payroll.
- Requests are made through PeopleDesk (category "Payroll & Compensation") and require line manager and Head of People & Culture approval.
- Any unpaid balance is deducted from the final settlement if employment ends before full repayment.

## 8. Payroll Error Handling

An employee who believes their pay is wrong must raise a PeopleDesk ticket in the category "Payroll & Compensation". Payroll errors are classified **P2**: first response within 1 working day and resolution within 5 working days.

| Situation | Action |
|---|---|
| Underpayment or error of SAR 1,000 or less | Corrected in the next payroll run, and in any case within 5 working days of the ticket where a correction can be processed |
| Underpayment or error greater than SAR 1,000 | An **off-cycle payment** is made so the employee is not left short before the next pay date |
| Overpayment | The employee is notified in writing; recovery is agreed with the employee and normally spread over up to 3 months, never exceeding what the law permits |

## 9. Annual Salary Review and Promotion Cycles

### 9.1 Salary review

The annual salary review takes place in **Q1** and any increase takes effect on **1 April**. Increases take into account the annual performance rating (see VND-HR-010), calibration outcomes, market data and budget. A review does not guarantee an increase. Employees who joined within the last 90 days before 1 April are normally not included until the next cycle.

### 9.2 Promotion cycles

Promotions are considered in two cycles each year, in **April** and **October**. A promotion changes grade and may change basic salary; criteria are set out in VND-HR-010.

## 10. Medical Insurance

### 10.1 Eligibility

| Employment type | Employee cover | Dependants |
|---|---|---|
| Full-time | From day 1 (statutory) | Spouse and up to 3 children, added after probation |
| Part-time | Employee only | Not eligible |
| Fixed-term/contractor | Not covered | Not eligible |
| Intern | Basic emergency coverage only | Not eligible |

### 10.2 Adding dependants

1. After the employee has completed probation, raise a PeopleDesk ticket in the category "Benefits".
2. Attach the required documents: a copy of the dependant's passport or national ID, the marriage certificate (spouse) or birth certificate (child), and, for residents of KSA or the UAE, the dependant's residence permit where applicable.
3. People & Culture submits the request to the insurer and confirms enrolment within **10 working days** of receiving complete documents.

Benefits tickets are P3 (first response in 2 working days).

## 11. Life and Disability Insurance

Full-time employees are covered by life and disability insurance after completing probation. Policy summaries and beneficiary nomination forms are available in VendHub.

## 12. End-of-Service Gratuity

### 12.1 Eligibility

End-of-service gratuity is payable to **full-time and part-time** employees only. Contractors and interns are not eligible.

### 12.2 Calculation

Gratuity accrues on basic salary only (not allowances) at:

- **half a month's basic salary** for each year of service in years 1 to 5; and
- **one month's basic salary** for each year of service after year 5;
- pro-rata for part years.

### 12.3 Resignation rule (Company simplification)

Where the employee resigns, the amount calculated in 12.2 is paid in these proportions:

| Completed service at resignation | Share of gratuity paid |
|---|---|
| Less than 2 years | None |
| 2 years to less than 5 years | One-third |
| 5 years to 10 years | Two-thirds |
| More than 10 years | Full |

### 12.4 Worked example (illustrative)

An FT employee with a basic salary of SAR 20,000 per month resigns after 7 years of service.

- Years 1 to 5: 5 x 0.5 = 2.5 months of basic.
- Years 6 and 7: 2 x 1 = 2 months of basic.
- Total accrued: 4.5 months x SAR 20,000 = SAR 90,000.
- Resignation at 5 to 10 years: two-thirds is payable = SAR 60,000.

The gratuity is paid with the final settlement within 14 days of the last day. The salary figure is an example only and does not indicate any actual pay band.

## 13. Learning and Development Budget

- **Amount:** SAR 3,000 per calendar year.
- **Eligibility:** full-time employees who have completed probation. Part-time, contractors and interns are not eligible.
- **No rollover:** unused budget does not carry into the next year.
- **How to claim:** submit the L&D form in VendHub; the line manager approves before the spend is incurred.
- **What qualifies:** books, online and in-person courses, conferences, and professional certifications with a clear link to the employee's role or career path (see VND-HR-010).
- **What does not qualify:** hardware, general subscriptions, leisure courses and anything reimbursable under another policy.

Study and exam leave (up to 5 working days per year) is governed by VND-HR-004.

## 14. Wellness Allowance and Employee Assistance Programme

### 14.1 Wellness allowance

Full-time employees receive a wellness allowance of **SAR 1,200 per year** for gym memberships and mental-health apps, claimed in Expensify with a receipt.

### 14.2 Employee Assistance Programme (EAP)

The EAP provides confidential counselling with **6 free sessions per year** for all employment types, including interns and contractors. Details are in VendHub. Use of the EAP is not reported to managers.

## 15. Equity, Referral Bonus and Mobile Allowance

### 15.1 Employee Stock Option Plan (ESOP)

Full-time employees at grade **L4 and above** receive an ESOP grant at hire. Options vest over **4 years** with a **1-year cliff**: no options vest before the first anniversary, and thereafter vesting continues under the grant agreement. Full-time employees in **L1 to L3** may receive discretionary grants at the annual review. Terms are set in the individual grant agreement.

### 15.2 Referral bonus

A current employee who refers a candidate who is hired receives **SAR 4,000** once the new hire completes probation. Members of People & Culture and the referred person's direct manager are not eligible.

### 15.3 Mobile phone allowance

A mobile allowance of SAR 150 per month is paid to employees in roles designated customer/field-facing (Sales, Customer Success, Field Operations and Directors). See VND-HR-006.

## 16. Benefits During Leave and Notice

- **Unpaid leave** (up to 30 days per year, approved under VND-HR-004): benefits continue; leave accrual pauses.
- **Notice period:** benefits, including medical insurance, continue until the employee's last day of employment. Access to benefits ends at the end of that day.

## 18. Letters and Frequently Asked Questions

### 18.1 Letters

Salary certificates, no-objection certificates (NOC) and similar letters are requested through PeopleDesk using category "Letter Request". They are issued within **3 working days**. State the addressee and purpose in the ticket.

### 18.2 Frequently asked questions

**When am I paid?** On the 25th of each month, or the preceding working day, by WPS bank transfer.

**How much can I take as a salary advance?** Up to 50% of one month's basic salary, once per calendar year, repaid over 3 months.

**My pay is SAR 1,400 short. What happens?** Raise a P2 PeopleDesk ticket. Because the error exceeds SAR 1,000, an off-cycle payment is made.

**Do my dependants get medical cover on day 1?** No. Full-time employees can add a spouse and up to 3 children after probation, via a PeopleDesk "Benefits" ticket; enrolment takes up to 10 working days.

**Can I carry over my L&D budget?** No. The SAR 3,000 budget does not roll over.

**Are benefits affected during my notice period or unpaid leave?** No. Benefits continue until the last day and throughout approved unpaid leave.

## 19. Roles and Responsibilities

- **Employees:** check payslips, report errors promptly, keep bank and dependant details current.
- **Line managers:** approve L&D and advance requests fairly and promptly; do not promise pay changes outside the review cycle.
- **People & Culture:** administer payroll, benefits and letters; hold salary bands; meet the P2 and P3 SLAs.
- **Finance & Administration:** fund and release payroll on time.
- **Head of People & Culture:** owns this policy and approves exceptions.

## 20. Exceptions and Escalation

Exceptions to this policy require written approval from the Head of People & Culture and, where cost is involved, the CFO. Disputes about pay or benefits are raised through PeopleDesk and, if unresolved, handled as a grievance under VND-HR-002.
