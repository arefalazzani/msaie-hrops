## 1. Purpose

This SOP sets out the step-by-step procedure for claiming and reimbursing business expenses at Vendelligent Technologies LLC ("Vendelligent", "the Company"). It implements VND-HR-006 (Business Travel and Expense Reimbursement Policy) and explains how to decide whether a cost is reimbursable, how to submit a claim in Expensify, how it is approved and audited, and when it is paid.

## 2. Scope

This SOP applies to Full-time, Part-time, Contractor and Intern staff in the Riyadh HQ, the Dubai office and the Remote Engineering Hub. It covers ordinary expense claims, travel claims, corporate-card reconciliation, home-office stipend claims and mileage claims. Amounts are in SAR. Dubai office expenses in AED are converted as described in section 11. Equipment is covered by VND-HR-007 and is not an expense.

## 3. Definitions

- **Expensify**: the expense tool, integrated with VendPay, in which all claims are submitted.
- **VendPay**: the payroll module inside VendHub that pays approved claims.
- **SecureVend**: the IT service desk that provides equipment and procures software.
- **Claim**: a submitted report in Expensify containing one or more expense lines.
- **Itemised receipt**: a receipt showing vendor, date, items and amounts.
- **Lost-receipt declaration**: a signed declaration used when a receipt under SAR 300 is lost.
- **Per diem**: a fixed daily allowance for meals and incidentals, with no receipts: SAR 250 per day domestic and SAR 350 per day international.
- **Travel request ID**: the identifier of the approved travel request, attached to travel claims.
- **Home-office stipend**: a one-time SAR 1,500 payment for full-time employees after probation.
- **Working day**: a day in the employee's Anchor Office working week, excluding public holidays.

### 6.1 Receipt rules

An itemised receipt is required for any expense of SAR 100 or more. For a lost receipt under SAR 300, a signed declaration may be used, up to a maximum of 3 declarations per employee per calendar year. A lost receipt of SAR 300 or more is not reimbursable unless the CFO approves an exception. Per diem needs no receipts.

### 6.2 Travel claims

Flights under 6 hours are economy; flights of 6 hours or more may be premium economy; business class requires CEO or CFO approval. Hotel caps per night are SAR 700 for Riyadh, Dubai and Doha, SAR 900 for Europe and the UK, SAR 1,000 for North America, and SAR 600 elsewhere. Ground transport by taxi or ride-hailing is reimbursed; car rental needs manager approval. Per diem is SAR 250 per day domestic or SAR 350 per day international. Meals claimed under per diem cannot also be claimed by receipt.

### 6.3 Corporate-card reconciliation

Each corporate-card holder must reconcile every card transaction in Expensify within 15 days of the transaction. Unreconciled items beyond 15 days are reported to the line manager and Finance, and repeated failure may lead to card suspension.

## 7. Approval, Rejection and Resubmission

Approvers must act within 3 working days. If an approver takes no action, Finance may escalate to the approver's manager.

| Rejection reason | What the employee must do |
|---|---|
| Missing receipt | Attach the receipt, or a declaration if eligible |
| Over policy cap | Resubmit only the eligible amount |
| Non-reimbursable item | Remove the line |
| Missing attendee list | Add attendees |
| Missing travel request ID | Attach the ID |
| Submitted after 30 days | Request an exception (section 13) |
| Wrong category | Correct and resubmit |

A rejected claim can be corrected and resubmitted. The 30-day period still counts from the original expense date, unless the Finance team confirms otherwise.

## 8. Finance Audit Sampling

Finance reviews 10% of claims, selected at random, and every claim above SAR 5,000. Auditors may request original documents. The reviewed claim may be returned to the approver. Audit results are reported to the CFO quarterly.

## 9. Home-Office Stipend Claim

1. Confirm eligibility: Full-time, past probation (hybrid or remote), not a Field Operations technician.
2. Buy the desk, chair or monitor needed for the home office.
3. Submit an Expensify claim with category "Home-office stipend". Items above SAR 500 need an itemised receipt.
4. The chair sub-cap is SAR 800. The total reimbursed may not exceed SAR 1,500, once per employee.
5. The line manager approves within 3 working days. Part-time, Contractor and Intern staff are not eligible.

## 10. Mileage Claim

1. Use mileage only for a personal car used for approved business travel.
2. Record date, start, destination, purpose and kilometres in Expensify.
3. The rate is SAR 1.0 per km. Commuting is not claimable.
4. The line manager approves. Attach a map or log if the claim is audited.

## 11. Currency Conversion

Foreign-currency expenses are converted using the Expensify rate on the expense date. Dubai office AED amounts convert at about 1 AED to 1.02 SAR. Policy limits are in SAR and apply after conversion. Card fees and exchange differences on personal cards are not reimbursed.

## 12. Fraud Flags and Consequences

Finance flags: duplicate receipts, altered receipts, claims for personal items, split claims to avoid approval thresholds, repeated lost-receipt declarations, and claims for travel not taken. Falsifying expenses is zero tolerance conduct under VND-HR-001. Consequences include rejection, repayment, disciplinary action under VND-HR-011 (up to dismissal), and where appropriate reporting to the authorities. Anyone may report concerns through SpeakUp.

## 13. Worked Example

Layla, a Sales Manager, returns from a 3-day client visit to Dubai. Her hotel is SAR 650 per night and the dinner for four clients costs SAR 1,100. She submits on day 10 with:

- Hotel for 2 nights: SAR 1,300 (cap SAR 700 per night) with receipt.
- Dinner: SAR 1,100 with attendee list (four attendees at SAR 275 per head, under the SAR 300 cap; total under SAR 1,500, so no Director approval for entertainment).
- Per diem: 3 days at SAR 350 for international travel = SAR 1,050.
- Taxi: SAR 180 with receipts.

Total: SAR 3,630. This is under SAR 5,000, so the line manager approves it within 3 working days. If approved on the 12th, payment is made in the next payroll on the 25th. If approved on the 17th, the claim is paid in the following payroll.

## 16. Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I expense a new monitor?**
No. Monitors are Company equipment provided through SecureVend.

**What is the deadline for submitting a claim?**
30 days after the expense.

**When will I be paid?**
In the next payroll (the 25th) if the claim is approved by the 15th.

**Who approves a claim of SAR 12,000?**
A Director, because the range SAR 5,001 to 20,000 is a Director approval.

**I lost a SAR 150 receipt. What do I do?**
Submit a signed lost-receipt declaration. You may use up to 3 declarations per year.

**Can I claim alcohol with a client dinner?**
No. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

## 17. Roles and Responsibilities

- **Employee**: follows the policy, submits accurate claims on time and keeps originals for 12 months.
- **Line manager**: approves up to SAR 5,000 and checks business purpose.
- **Director**: approves SAR 5,001 to 20,000 and client entertainment above SAR 1,500.
- **CFO**: approves claims above SAR 20,000 and owns audit results.
- **Finance & Administration**: audits, pays through VendPay and investigates fraud flags.
- **People & Culture**: supports disciplinary follow-up and owns this SOP.

## 18. Exceptions and Escalation

Exceptions to this SOP, such as late claims or lost receipts of SAR 300 or more, are requested through PeopleDesk and approved by the CFO. Disputes over approval decisions go to the approver's manager, then to the Head of People & Culture. Suspected fraud is escalated to the CFO and the Head of People & Culture immediately.
