## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out every type of leave available at Vendelligent Technologies LLC ("Vendelligent", "the Company"), the amount of leave each employment type receives, how leave is accrued, requested, approved and recorded, and how leave interacts with public holidays, part-time working and resignation. It reflects the values **Respect by default** and **Own the outcome**: employees are expected to rest and to plan their absences so that customers, brand partners and teammates are not left exposed.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to full-time (FT) and part-time (PT) employees in the Riyadh HQ, the Dubai office and the Remote Engineering Hub, to interns (IN) and to fixed-term contractors (CT). Hardware & Field Operations technicians are covered, subject to the regional blackout rule in Section 7.6. The step-by-step request procedure is in **VND-SOP-103 PTO Request and Approval SOP**. All figures in this policy are working days per full calendar year (1 January to 31 December) for a full-time employee unless stated otherwise.

## 3. Definitions

- **Annual leave (PTO)**: paid time off for rest and personal use.
- **Working day**: a day on which the employee is scheduled to work under their Anchor Office calendar (Sunday to Thursday for Riyadh, Monday to Friday for Dubai); see VND-HR-003.
- **Probation**: the first 90 calendar days of employment for FT and PT employees, extendable once by up to 90 days (maximum 180 days in total).
- **Director**: a Grade L5 leader, such as the Director of Hardware & Field Operations or the Director of Engineering.
- **VendHub**: the people portal where all leave requests are made and balances are shown.
- **PeopleDesk**: the HR case system for questions and exceptions.

## 4. General Rules

- Leave requests are made **only through VendHub**. Requests by email, Slack or verbally are not valid and do not create an entitlement.
- PeopleDesk is used for questions and exceptions, not for requesting leave.
- Leave is counted in working days. Weekends and public holidays of the employee's Anchor Office are not counted.
- Leave must not be taken before it has been approved in VendHub, except for sick leave and emergencies as described in Sections 6 and 11.
- The AI HR Assistant may explain this policy but cannot approve leave or change leave records.

## 5. Annual Leave (PTO)

### 5.1 Entitlement and accrual

- Full-time employees receive **22 working days** of annual leave per year.
- After **5 completed years of service**, entitlement rises to **30 working days** per year.
- Leave accrues from day 1 at approximately **1.83 days per month** for the 22-day annual entitlement, increasing to **2.5 days per month** after 5 completed years of service for the 30-day entitlement.
- Part-time employees accrue pro-rata (see Section 12).

### 5.2 Probation restriction

Although leave accrues from day 1, it **cannot be taken during the first 60 days** of employment, except for an emergency approved by the Head of People & Culture. After day 60, accrued leave may be taken with approval, even though probation continues until day 90.

### 5.3 Balance rules

- **No negative balance** is permitted. VendHub rejects any request that exceeds the available balance.
- Employees should check their balance in VendHub before requesting.

### 5.4 Carry-over

- A maximum of **5 days** may be carried over into the next calendar year.
- Carried-over days must be used by **31 March**, or they are forfeited.
- Days above the 5-day cap are forfeited on 31 December.
- Managers must allow employees reasonable opportunity to take their leave during the year.

### 5.5 No cash-out

Annual leave cannot be exchanged for cash during employment. The only exception is **termination of employment**, when accrued, unused leave is paid out at final settlement (see Section 14).

## 6. Requesting and Approving Annual Leave

### 6.1 Notice periods

| Length of request | Minimum notice | Approvals required |
|---|---|---|
| 1 to 2 working days | At least 3 working days | Line manager |
| 3 to 5 working days | At least 10 working days | Line manager |
| More than 5 consecutive working days | At least 20 working days | Line manager and Director |
| More than 15 consecutive working days | At least 20 working days | Line manager, Director and CEO or COO |

The maximum period of consecutive leave is **15 working days** unless the CEO or COO approves a longer period.

### 6.2 Manager approval timeline

The line manager must approve or decline a request in VendHub within **2 working days**. If there is no action after **3 working days**, VendHub automatically escalates the request to the manager's manager. A manager who declines a request must give a reason and, wherever possible, suggest alternative dates.

### 6.3 Short-notice requests

A request that does not meet the notice period may still be approved by the line manager (and Director where applicable) if business needs allow, but is not an entitlement. Genuine emergencies should be raised with the manager immediately and recorded in VendHub afterwards.

## 7. Blackout Periods

Managers may decline leave during the following blackout periods:

| Department | Blackout rule |
|---|---|
| Hardware & Field Operations | No more than 25% of a regional team may be on leave in the same week. |
| Finance & Administration | The last 5 working days of each quarter. |
| Engineering & AI Platform | Launch freeze weeks announced by the CTO. |

Other departments have no standing blackout, but managers may ask employees to avoid specific dates for critical launches with Smart Machines or brand-partner commitments, giving at least 20 working days' notice. Leave already approved before a blackout is announced is not withdrawn except in an emergency approved by a Director.

## 8. Sick Leave

- Employees are entitled to up to **30 working days of sick leave per year**: the first **15 days at 100% pay** and the next **15 days at 50% pay**.
- Sick leave is **not accrued** and the entitlement **resets on 1 January** each year. Unused sick leave does not carry over.
- The employee must notify their line manager **before 10:00 on the first day** of absence and record the absence in VendHub.
- A **medical certificate** from a licensed practitioner is required for absence of **more than 2 consecutive days**.
- Medical details are confidential. Employees should upload certificates to VendHub, not send them to managers. Sensitive medical matters are handled by People & Culture as P1 or P2 PeopleDesk tickets.
- Sick leave that exceeds 30 days is treated as unpaid and discussed with People & Culture. Annual leave may be used by agreement.
- If an employee falls ill during approved annual leave and provides a medical certificate, the sick days may be recorded as sick leave and the annual leave restored.

## 9. Parental Leave

### 9.1 Maternity leave

- **10 weeks at full pay**, of which at least **6 weeks must be taken after the birth**.
- A further **4 weeks of unpaid extension** may be requested.
- Eligibility starts **after probation** is completed.
- The employee should notify People & Culture through PeopleDesk as early as possible, and no later than the 28th week of pregnancy where practicable, and submit a medical certificate with the expected delivery date.

### 9.2 Paternity leave

- **5 working days at full pay**, to be taken **within 30 days of the birth**.
- Requests are made in VendHub with a copy of the birth notification once available.

|---|---|---|

| Marriage leave | 5 working days, once | Paid; one-time entitlement during employment |
| Hajj leave | 10 working days, once | Paid; after 2 years of service; one-time entitlement during employment |

### 10.1 Unpaid leave

During unpaid leave, benefits (such as medical insurance) continue, but annual leave accrual pauses and no salary is paid. Unpaid leave of up to 30 days per year may be approved when the employee has no available paid leave or has a personal need. It is not an entitlement.

### 10.2 Hajj leave

Hajj leave may be taken once during employment, after 2 years of service. Requests should be made at least 20 working days in advance, because the dates of Hajj are fixed by the authorities.

## 11. Emergencies

If an employee cannot work because of an emergency (for example a family emergency or a serious incident at home), they must contact their line manager as soon as possible, and no later than 10:00 on the first day. The manager records the absence in VendHub and the employee may use annual leave, bereavement leave or unpaid leave as appropriate. During the first 60 days, emergency annual leave must be approved by the Head of People & Culture.

## 12. Public Holidays, Part-Time and Pro-Rata

- A **public holiday that falls during approved leave is not deducted** from the leave balance. For example, if an employee anchored to Riyadh takes leave across Eid al-Fitr, the public holiday days are not counted as leave. The holiday calendar is in VND-HR-003.
- Part-time employees (20 to 32 hours per week) accrue annual leave **pro-rata** to their contracted hours relative to 40 hours. For example, a part-time employee working 20 hours a week accrues 50% of the full-time entitlement.
- Other entitlements (maternity, paternity, bereavement, marriage, Hajj, study) follow the stated number of days; for part-time employees, the days are working days on their contracted schedule.
- Unpaid leave pauses accrual.

## 13. Interns and Contractors

- **Interns** (3 to 6 month programme) receive **1 day of leave per month** of the programme.
- **Contractors** (fixed-term, up to 12 months) have **no leave accrual** under this policy. Time off is agreed with the project owner under the contractor agreement and is unpaid.

## 14. Leave and Resignation or Termination

- Employees who resign remain entitled to take approved leave during the notice period, subject to manager approval and business need. Notice is 1 week during probation, and after probation 30 days (L1 to L3) or 60 days (L4+).
- Leave taken during a notice period does not extend the notice period unless the leave is unpaid and agreed in writing; managers may ask employees to use remaining leave before the last day.
- On termination for any reason, **accrued, unused annual leave is paid out** at final settlement, which is made within 14 days of the last day of employment.
- Leave taken beyond accrued balance is not permitted (no negative balance). Where an error has occurred, any overpaid amount may be deducted from final settlement after notifying the employee.
- Sick leave and other leave types are not paid out.

## 16. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1. How many days of annual leave do I get?**
Full-time employees receive 22 working days a year, rising to 30 after 5 completed years. Leave accrues from day 1 at approximately 1.83 days per month, increasing to 2.5 days per month after 5 completed years.

**Q2. I joined three weeks ago. Can I take leave?**
Not for the first 60 days, except for an emergency approved by the Head of People & Culture.

**Q3. Can I carry over unused leave?**
Yes, up to 5 days, which must be used by 31 March or they are forfeited.

**Q4. How much notice do I need for a one-week holiday?**
A one-week holiday of 5 working days requires at least 10 working days' notice and line manager approval. Requests exceeding 5 consecutive working days require at least 20 working days' notice and approval from the line manager and Director.

**Q5. Can I ask for my unused leave to be paid out?**
No, only at termination of employment, when accrued, unused leave is paid at final settlement.

**Q6. My manager has not responded to my request. What happens?**
Managers should approve within 2 working days. After 3 working days without action, VendHub escalates the request to the manager's manager.

**Q7. Is a public holiday counted if it falls during my leave?**
No. Public holidays of your Anchor Office are not deducted from your balance.

**Q8. When do I need a medical certificate?**
For sick leave of more than 2 consecutive days. You must notify your manager before 10:00 on day one.

## 17. Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Employee | Request leave in VendHub within the notice periods, check balances, arrange handover, notify sick absence before 10:00. |
| Line manager | Decide requests within 2 working days, apply blackout rules fairly, keep coverage for Smart Machine operations and brand partners. |
| Director | Approve leave longer than 5 consecutive working days and unpaid leave. |
| CEO / COO | Approve leave longer than 15 consecutive working days. |
| People & Culture | Maintain balances, handle exceptions and parental leave, pay out accrued leave at termination. |
| Head of People & Culture | Own this policy, approve emergency leave in the first 60 days, and co-approve unpaid leave. |

## 18. Exceptions and Escalation

Exceptions (for example an extension to the 31 March carry-over deadline for an employee on long-term sick leave) must be requested through PeopleDesk under "Leave & Attendance" and approved by the Head of People & Culture. Disagreements with a manager's decision may be escalated to the manager's manager and then to People & Culture, or raised as a grievance under VND-HR-002. Procedure for requests and approvals is in VND-SOP-103.
