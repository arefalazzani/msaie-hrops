## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out the rules for business travel and for the reimbursement of business expenses at Vendelligent Technologies LLC ("Vendelligent", "the Company"). It ensures that employees travel and spend in a way that is reasonable, approved in advance where required, properly documented and consistent with the value **Own the outcome**. The step-by-step claim procedure is in VND-SOP-105 Expense Claim and Reimbursement SOP.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees who incur business expenses on behalf of the Company, including travel to client sites, brand-partner meetings, Smart Machine installation sites, the Riyadh HQ and the Dubai office. Eligibility for individual allowances (home-office stipend, mobile allowance, corporate card) varies by employment type and role and is stated in the relevant section. All amounts in this policy are in Saudi Riyals (SAR). Expenses incurred at the Dubai office in AED are converted at 1 AED ≈ 1.02 SAR.

## 3. Definitions

- **Business expense**: a reasonable cost wholly and necessarily incurred for Company business and approved under this policy.
- **Travel request**: the pre-approval request submitted in VendHub before any business trip.
- **TravelDesk**: the Company travel desk, reached at the TravelDesk email alias, which books flights, hotels and visas on behalf of employees.
- **Per diem**: a fixed daily allowance for meals and incidentals while travelling, paid without receipts.
- **Expensify**: the expense tool used to submit claims, integrated with VendPay.
- **Domestic travel**: travel within the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia (or within the UAE for Dubai-based employees) that involves an overnight stay. **International travel**: all other overnight travel outside the employee's country of work.
- **Line manager, Director, CFO**: the approvers named in the approval tiers in Section 14.

## 4. Principles

- Employees must spend Company money as carefully as they would spend their own, and choose the most cost-effective option that meets the business need.
- Travel must be approved before it is booked. Expenses that were not pre-approved when approval is required may be refused.
- Every claim must be honest, accurate and supported by the documents this policy requires. Falsifying an expense is gross misconduct (see Section 21).
- Employees may not approve their own expenses or those of anyone to whom they report.
- Personal and business costs must not be mixed on a single receipt without clearly separating them in the claim.

## 5. Who May Incur Expenses

- Full-time and Part-time employees may incur business expenses within this policy.
- Fixed-term/Contractor employees may incur expenses only for the project named in their contractor agreement and only with prior written approval from their line manager.
- Interns may incur expenses only when accompanying or directed by their manager, and only with that manager's prior approval in VendHub.
- Allowances that are limited to certain groups (home-office stipend, mobile allowance, corporate card) are governed by Sections 11, 12 and 16.

## 6. Pre-Approval and Booking of Travel

### 6.1 Travel request deadlines

All business trips requiring an overnight stay must be approved through a travel request in VendHub **before** any booking is made.

| Trip type | Minimum lead time for the travel request | Approver |
|---|---|---|
| Domestic | At least 7 working days before departure | Line manager |
| International | At least 14 working days before departure | Line manager, plus Director for trips over 5 nights |

Requests submitted later than these lead times require a written reason and the Director's approval. Urgent client or Smart Machine outage travel may be approved by the Director of Hardware & Field Operations or the COO on the same day, with the request completed in VendHub afterwards.

### 6.2 Booking channel

- Flights, hotels and visas must be booked through **TravelDesk** (email alias: TravelDesk, quoting the approved travel request number).
- An employee may **self-book** only if the line manager approves self-booking in the travel request. Self-booked travel must still respect the flight class and hotel caps in this policy, and the receipt must be uploaded to Expensify.
- Non-refundable bookings made before the travel request is approved are at the employee's own risk.

## 7. Flights

| Flight duration (one way) | Permitted class | Approval |
|---|---|---|
| Under 6 hours | Economy | None beyond the travel request |
| 6 hours or more | Premium economy | None beyond the travel request |
| Any duration | Business class | Only the CEO and CFO may travel business class; any other employee needs CEO or CFO approval |

Upgrades paid for by the employee are not reimbursed. Upgrades using personal loyalty points are the employee's own choice and are not a Company cost.

## 8. Accommodation

Hotel stays are reimbursed up to the nightly cap for the destination region. Caps include taxes and service charges but exclude the mini-bar, which is never reimbursable.

| Destination region | Cap per night |
|---|---|
| Riyadh, Dubai, Doha | SAR 700 |
| Europe and United Kingdom | SAR 900 |
| North America | SAR 1,000 |
| All other destinations | SAR 600 |

Amounts above the cap are borne by the employee unless the line manager approved a higher rate in advance in the travel request, for example when a conference block rate applies. Laundry is reimbursable only on trips of 5 nights or more.

## 9. Per Diem

- The per diem is **SAR 250 per day for domestic travel** and **SAR 350 per day for international travel**.
- It covers meals, snacks, beverages, tips and small incidentals (for example local SIM cards, bottled water and tolls). No receipts are needed for per diem.
- It is paid for each full travel day. Where a meal is provided by the host, client or conference, the employee should record this in the claim and the line manager may reduce the per diem for that day proportionally.
- Per diem is not paid for days spent at the employee's normal place of work, or for days of leave taken during a trip.
- Alcohol is not covered by the per diem and is never reimbursable.

## 10. Ground Transport and Mileage

- Taxis and ride-hailing (such as Uber or Careem) used for business, including trips to a client or brand-partner location, are reimbursable with the app receipt.
- Car rental requires line manager approval in the travel request. The cheapest suitable class must be chosen, and fuel is reimbursable with receipts.
- Use of a personal car for business is reimbursed at **SAR 1.0 per kilometre**, based on the distance recorded in the claim. Commuting between home and the normal place of work is not reimbursable.
- Traffic fines, parking fines and similar penalties are never reimbursed.

## 11. Visas and Travel Insurance

- Business visa fees are reimbursable. The employee must give TravelDesk their passport details at least 14 working days before international departure, because visa processing may take longer than the travel request lead time.
- The Company provides business travel insurance for approved trips booked through TravelDesk. Self-booked trips must be registered with TravelDesk so cover applies.
- Leisure extensions to a business trip are at the employee's own cost and require manager approval of the dates.

## 12. Client Entertainment

- Client and brand-partner entertainment is limited to **SAR 300 per head**.
- The claim must list all attendees (names and companies).
- Where the total exceeds **SAR 1,500**, prior Director approval is required.
- Alcohol and personal entertainment are not reimbursable.

## 13. Allowances, Stipends and Software

### 13.1 Mobile allowance

Employees in roles designated "customer/field-facing" (Sales, Customer Success, Field Operations and Directors) receive a mobile allowance of **SAR 150 per month**, paid through VendPay. It is not claimed through Expensify.

### 13.2 Home-office stipend

- A one-time **SAR 1,500** stipend is available to Full-time employees (hybrid or remote) **after probation**, for a desk, chair or monitor.
- Items costing more than **SAR 500** need an itemised receipt. A chair is subject to a sub-cap of **SAR 800**.
- The stipend is **not available** to Field Operations technicians, Part-time employees, Fixed-term/Contractor employees or Interns.
- Items bought with the stipend belong to the employee (see VND-HR-007 Equipment, IT Assets and Home Office Policy).

### 13.3 Software and subscriptions

Software and subscriptions must be bought through SecureVend procurement and not expensed, unless the purchase is a one-off cost **under SAR 200**.

## 14. Receipts, Submission and Approval

### 14.1 Receipts

- An itemised receipt is required for every expense of **SAR 100 or more**. Per diem needs no receipt.
- For a lost receipt for an expense **under SAR 300**, the employee may submit a signed declaration. A maximum of **3 lost-receipt declarations** is allowed per calendar year.

### 14.2 Submission deadline

Claims must be submitted in Expensify within **30 days** of the expense date. Late claims may be rejected unless the Director approves an exception.

### 14.3 Approval tiers

| Claim amount (per claim) | Approver |
|---|---|
| Up to SAR 5,000 | Line manager |
| SAR 5,001 to SAR 20,000 | Director |
| More than SAR 20,000 | CFO |

### 14.4 Payment timing

Approved claims are reimbursed in the next payroll run through VendPay if approved by the **15th** of the month. Claims approved after the 15th are paid in the following month's payroll. Payroll is paid on the 25th (or the preceding working day).

## 15. Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following are not reimbursable:

- alcohol and the mini-bar;
- fines and penalties;
- personal entertainment;
- upgrades without approval;
- family travel and the costs of travelling companions;
- laundry on trips shorter than 5 nights;
- commuting costs, personal grooming and personal clothing.

## 16. Corporate Cards

- Employees at grade **L4 and above** (Lead/Manager, Director, Executive) may hold a Company corporate card, issued by Finance & Administration on the line manager's or Director's request.
- Corporate cards are for business expenses only. **Personal use is prohibited.** Personal charges must be repaid in full within 7 days and may lead to card withdrawal and disciplinary action.
- Every card transaction must be matched with a receipt in Expensify within 30 days of the transaction.
- Lost or stolen cards must be reported to Finance & Administration and to SecureVend immediately.

## 17. Field Operations Site Visits

Field Operations technicians travel to retail locations to install and maintain Smart Machines. Specific rules apply:

- **Tools and consumables**: toolkit items and small consumables bought on site for an urgent repair are reimbursable with a receipt, up to SAR 500 per visit without prior approval; larger purchases need the Director of Hardware & Field Operations' approval.
- **PPE**: personal protective equipment required by a site is issued by the Company; if a technician must buy PPE in an emergency, the cost is reimbursable with a receipt.
- **Fuel**: fuel for Company vehicles is reimbursed with receipts or paid on the Company fuel card. Fuel for a personal car used for site visits is paid as mileage at SAR 1.0/km.
- Per diem applies to site visits that require an overnight stay.

## 19. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1. How early must I request a domestic trip?** At least 7 working days before departure, via a travel request in VendHub.

**Q2. May I fly premium economy to Frankfurt?** Yes, if the one-way flight is 6 hours or more. For shorter flights, economy applies. Business class is for the CEO and CFO only, unless the CEO or CFO approves otherwise.

**Q3. I lost a taxi receipt for SAR 120. What do I do?** Because the amount is under SAR 300, submit a signed lost-receipt declaration. Remember you may use only 3 per calendar year.

**Q4. Can I claim a per diem and also claim my meals?** No. The per diem (SAR 250 domestic, SAR 350 international) covers meals and incidentals, and receipts for meals are not reimbursed separately.

**Q5. My claim was approved on the 18th. When am I paid?** After the 15th cut-off, so it is paid in the following month's payroll.

**Q6. Can I buy a SAR 450 software tool on my own card?** No. Software of SAR 200 or more must go through SecureVend procurement.

## 20. Roles and Responsibilities

- **Employees**: obtain pre-approval, follow the caps, keep receipts, submit within 30 days and make truthful claims.
- **Line managers and Directors**: review claims against this policy, approve within their tier and refuse non-compliant items.
- **CFO and Finance & Administration**: approve claims above SAR 20,000, issue corporate cards, audit claims and run payment through VendPay.
- **TravelDesk**: books approved travel, arranges visas and registers insurance.
- **People & Culture**: owns this policy and answers questions through PeopleDesk.

## 21. Exceptions and Escalation

Exceptions to a cap or deadline must be approved in writing in advance by the Director (up to SAR 5,000 above the standard) or by the CFO (above that), and recorded in the travel request or the claim. Falsifying an expense, claiming a personal cost as a business cost or submitting a duplicate claim is **gross misconduct** and is handled under VND-HR-011 Disciplinary Policy and Procedure, which may result in dismissal without prior warnings and recovery of the amounts paid. Suspected fraud may be reported through the anonymous SpeakUp channel. Questions are raised through PeopleDesk under the category "Expenses".
