## 1. Purpose

This SOP describes how an employee of Vendelligent Technologies LLC ("Vendelligent", "the Company") requests, gets approved for, prepares for and carries out a period of Work from Anywhere (WFA), meaning working remotely from a country other than the country of their Anchor Office. It implements VND-HR-005 (Remote, Hybrid and Work-from-Anywhere Policy).

## 2. Scope

This SOP applies to Full-time (FT) and Part-time (PT) employees of the Riyadh HQ, the Dubai office and the Remote Engineering Hub. It covers the standard path (up to 20 working days per calendar year, from an Approved Country) and the extended path (exceptions). Contractors and Interns are not eligible for WFA. Field Operations technicians, who are site-based, are not eligible. Working remotely within the country of the Anchor Office follows the hybrid rules in VND-HR-005.

## 3. Definitions

- **WFA**: working from a country other than that of the employee's Anchor Office.
- **Anchor Office**: the office (Riyadh or Dubai) whose calendar the employee follows, shown in VendHub.
- **WFA allowance**: 20 working days per calendar year, shown as remaining days in VendHub.
- **Approved Country**: a country on the 2026 list in section 5.
- **Restricted country**: any country under comprehensive sanctions, or where Company VPN or Google Workspace are blocked. WFA there is never approved.
- **Visitor status**: the employee's valid right to be in the country as a visitor. The Company does not sponsor visas for WFA.
- **Core hours**: 10:00 to 15:00 Anchor Office time.
- **PIP**: Performance Improvement Plan (60 days).
- **Working day**: a day in the Anchor Office working week excluding public holidays.
- **Restricted data**: data in the highest class under VND-HR-008, including consumer PII from Smart Machines, payment data and employee records.

## 5. Eligibility and Country Check

### 5.1 Eligibility

| Condition | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Employment type | FT or PT only |
| Role | Not Field Operations |
| Probation | Completed (90 calendar days, up to 180 if extended) |
| Performance and conduct | No active PIP and no active disciplinary warning |
| Allowance | Days remaining in the 20-day annual allowance shown in VendHub cover the trip |

### 5.2 Approved Countries (2026)

Saudi Arabia, UAE, Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar, Egypt, Jordan, Turkey, United Kingdom, Ireland, Germany, France, Spain, Portugal, Netherlands, Italy, Poland, Canada, United States, India, Pakistan, Malaysia, Singapore, South Africa.

### 5.3 Restricted rule

A Restricted country is never approved. No Smart Machine production data may be accessed from a restricted location. A country that is simply not on the list is "non-approved" and follows the extended path (section 8).

## 8. Extended Path

An extended request must be raised through the WFA form, selecting "extended", plus a PeopleDesk case (Remote Work & WFA, P3). People & Culture, Legal & Compliance and Finance (for tax) each review it. The review takes 10 working days. The request must explain the business need, the tax and legal implications and the security plan. Extended requests are generally not granted. The decision is issued by the Head of People & Culture in writing, and an approval does not set a precedent. Employees must not book non-refundable travel before approval.

## 9. Security Pre-Departure Checklist

- [ ] The device is a Company-managed laptop; SecureVend device check completed
- [ ] VPN tested from the destination or a test connection
- [ ] Okta MFA working on the device and the registered phone
- [ ] Screen lock set to 5 minutes or less
- [ ] No Restricted data stored offline
- [ ] No customer or consumer data on personal devices
- [ ] Laptop is carried personally and never shipped
- [ ] No public Wi-Fi without VPN, and no printing of confidential data
- [ ] Time-zone plan agreed with the manager

## 10. Insurance

Emergency medical cover abroad applies for up to 30 days per trip for approved WFA. It does not extend to trips beyond 30 consecutive days. Employees should confirm cover details in VendHub before travel and carry the insurance card.

## 11. During the Trip

- Update the Slack status daily to show the country and availability.
- Be available during core hours, 10:00 to 15:00 Anchor Office time.
- Report any suspected breach, lost or stolen device to the SecureVend security queue and Slack #security-incidents within 1 hour of discovery.
- Contact the line manager and People & Culture if a medical, immigration or safety event affects the trip.
- Confirm the return date in VendHub.

## 14. Worked Example

Omar, a Full-time engineer in the Remote Engineering Hub, wants to work from Turkey for 6 weeks (about 30 working days) to be with family. He has used 4 WFA days this year, so 16 remain.

- Turkey is an Approved Country.
- Six weeks is more than the 16 remaining days and more than the 20-day annual allowance.
- It is also more than 30 consecutive calendar days.
- The request therefore goes on the extended path, with People & Culture, Legal & Compliance and Finance reviewing it over 10 working days. It is likely to be refused.

Suggested alternatives: split the stay, for example 16 working days now using the remaining allowance, and use annual leave (VND-SOP-103) for the rest; or reduce the trip to 16 working days or less and apply on the standard path.

## 17. Frequently Asked Questions

**How many WFA days do I get?**
Up to 20 working days per calendar year.

**How much notice is needed?**
At least 10 working days before travel, via the VendHub WFA form.

**Can I work from a country that is not on the list?**
Only through the extended path, and it is generally not granted. Restricted countries are never approved.

**Can I post my laptop ahead?**
No. Equipment must not be shipped internationally; carry it personally.

**Do I need a visa?**
You must hold a valid right to be in the country as a visitor. The Company does not sponsor visas for WFA.

**Can I work from a cafe?**
Only with VPN on a Company laptop, never on public Wi-Fi without VPN.

## 18. Roles and Responsibilities

- **Employee**: meets eligibility, submits on time, follows security rules and checks in daily.
- **Line manager**: decides within 3 working days and ensures coverage of core hours.
- **People & Culture**: advises on eligibility, runs extended reviews and owns this SOP.
- **Legal & Compliance**: reviews legal and visa matters for extended requests.
- **Finance**: reviews tax implications for extended requests.
- **SecureVend**: performs device checks and handles security incidents.
- **Head of People & Culture**: decides extended requests.

## 19. Exceptions and Escalation

Exceptions follow the extended path in section 8. A refused standard request may be appealed to the manager's manager within 5 working days. Security incidents are escalated to SecureVend and the CTO immediately. Disputes on eligibility are decided by the Head of People & Culture.
