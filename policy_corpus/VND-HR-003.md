## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out when Vendelligent Technologies LLC ("Vendelligent", "the Company") expects employees to work, how working time is recorded, how absences and lateness are handled, how overtime is authorised and paid, and which public holidays apply in 2026. It supports the value **Own the outcome** by giving every employee and manager clear, testable rules, and the value **Respect by default** by protecting rest, prayer and religious observance.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees of the Riyadh HQ, the Dubai office and the Remote Engineering Hub, including Hardware & Field Operations technicians who install and maintain Smart Machines in retail locations. Interns follow the working week of their office. Contractors (fixed-term, up to 12 months) follow the hours stated in their contractor agreement, but must observe the core hours rule in Section 4.2 when the agreement requires collaboration with Company teams. Leave entitlements are covered in VND-HR-004 and remote work in VND-HR-005.

## 3. Definitions

- **Anchor Office**: the office (Riyadh or Dubai) whose calendar an employee follows, shown in VendHub as *Anchor Office*. Remote Engineering Hub employees are each assigned one.
- **Core hours**: 10:00 to 15:00 local time (Anchor Office time), when every employee must be available.
- **Standard day / week**: 8 working hours per day and 40 working hours per week for full-time employees.
- **Non-exempt role**: a role eligible for overtime pay, mainly Field Operations technicians.
- **Exempt role**: engineers, managers and other professional staff, who do not receive overtime pay.
- **TOIL**: time off in lieu of overtime pay.
- **VendHub**: the Company people portal (HRIS) used for time, attendance and leave records.

## 4. Working Hours

### 4.1 Working week by office

| Office | Working week | Weekend |
|---|---|---|
| Riyadh HQ (KSA) | Sunday to Thursday | Friday and Saturday |
| Dubai office (UAE) | Monday to Friday | Saturday and Sunday |
| Remote Engineering Hub | Follows the employee's Anchor Office | Follows the Anchor Office |

An employee's Anchor Office is fixed in VendHub and is changed only through a PeopleDesk request approved by the Head of People & Culture.

### 4.2 Core hours and flexible start

- Core hours are **10:00 to 15:00 local time**. Employees must be available on Slack, calendar and video during core hours.
- Employees may start work at any time between **07:30 and 10:00** and finish after completing their 8-hour day, including breaks as set out in Section 4.4.
- Meetings involving more than one team should be scheduled inside core hours.
- A manager may agree a regular start time with an employee, and must record it as a note in VendHub.

### 4.3 Standard day and week

The standard working day is 8 hours and the standard working week is 40 hours for full-time employees. Part-time employees work 20 to 32 hours per week as stated in their contract. Hours are counted excluding unpaid meal breaks.

### 4.4 Breaks

- Employees working more than 5 hours in a day are entitled to a break of at least 30 minutes, which is not counted as working time unless the employee remains on duty.
- Short rest breaks during the day are encouraged and do not need to be recorded.
- Employees must have at least 11 hours of rest between the end of one working day and the start of the next, except for approved on-call duty (Section 9).

### 4.5 Ramadan hours

During Ramadan, the working day is reduced to **6 hours** (30 hours per week) for all employees in the KSA and UAE offices, with no reduction in pay. Core hours during Ramadan are announced by People & Culture in VendHub at least one week before Ramadan begins. Employees whose Anchor Office is in KSA or UAE follow this rule wherever they are working. Field Operations schedules are adjusted by the Director of Hardware & Field Operations so that site access times agreed with retail partners are still met.

## 5. Time Recording and Attendance

### 5.1 Recording time

- Employees must log hybrid office days in VendHub on or before the day they work in the office (see VND-HR-005 for the hybrid model).
- Non-exempt employees must record start time, finish time and break time daily in VendHub.
- Exempt employees record office days and absences; they are not required to record daily hours but must meet their 40-hour week and core hours.
- Managers must review and approve their team's time records in VendHub by the end of each month so that VendPay can process them.

### 5.2 Absence notification

- An employee who cannot attend work must notify their line manager **before 10:00** on the first day of absence, by phone, Slack or the agreed team channel, and must record the absence in VendHub on the same day or the first day back.
- Absence because of illness follows the sick leave rules in VND-HR-004 (medical certificate required for more than 2 consecutive days).
- An absence that is not notified and not approved is an **unreported absence**.

### 5.3 Unreported absences

Three unreported absences in a rolling 30-day period trigger an **attendance review** led by the line manager and People & Culture. The review considers the reasons for the absences, any support needed (including the Employee Assistance Programme, which offers 6 free confidential sessions a year), and whether the Disciplinary Policy (VND-HR-011) applies. Absence of more than 3 consecutive working days without any contact is treated as potential abandonment of work and is escalated to the Head of People & Culture immediately.

### 5.4 Lateness

- Because start times are flexible between 07:30 and 10:00, lateness means arriving after 10:00 without prior notice, or missing a scheduled in-person commitment such as an anchor-day meeting.
- Employees who expect to be late must tell their manager before 10:00.
- Repeated lateness (more than 3 occurrences in a calendar month) is discussed in a documented one-to-one and may lead to an informal improvement plan. Persistent lateness may be handled under VND-HR-011.

## 6. Overtime

### 6.1 Non-exempt roles

- Overtime must be **pre-approved by the line manager** before it is worked. Unapproved overtime is not payable, although it may be a performance matter.
- Overtime on a normal working day is paid at **150% of the employee's hourly rate**.
- Work on a weekend day or public holiday is paid at **200% of the hourly rate**, or taken as **time off in lieu (TOIL)** at the employee's choice. TOIL must be taken within 3 months of being earned, by agreement with the manager.
- Approved overtime is recorded in VendHub and paid through VendPay in the next payroll cycle (payroll runs on the 25th of each month).

### 6.2 Exempt roles

Engineers, managers and professional staff in exempt roles do not receive overtime pay or TOIL. Managers must nonetheless avoid routine work beyond the standard 40-hour week. Where sustained extra hours are required (for example during a launch freeze announced by the CTO), the manager must discuss workload and recovery time with the employee and may agree informal time off.

## 7. Public Holidays

### 7.1 Following your Anchor Office

Employees **follow the public holidays of their Anchor Office**. A Remote Hub employee anchored to Riyadh observes the KSA holidays; one anchored to Dubai observes the UAE holidays. Working from another country under the Work-from-Anywhere rules (VND-HR-005) does not change the holidays that apply.

### 7.2 2026 public holidays

|---|---|---|
| New Year | Not a public holiday | 1 January |
| Founding Day | 22 February | Not a public holiday |
| Eid al-Fitr | Approx. 19 to 23 March (4 working days) | Approx. 19 to 22 March |
| Arafat Day and Eid al-Adha | Approx. 26 to 29 May (4 working days) | Approx. 26 to 29 May |
| Islamic New Year | Not a public holiday | Approx. 16 June |
| Prophet's Birthday | Not a public holiday | Approx. 25 August |
| National Day | 23 September | 2 and 3 December |
| Commemoration Day | Not a public holiday | 1 December |

Dates marked "approx." depend on moon sighting and government announcement.

### 7.3 Confirmation of Islamic holiday dates

Islamic holiday dates are confirmed by government announcement. People & Culture posts the confirmed dates in VendHub **at least 48 hours before** the holiday begins. Managers must plan coverage on the basis of the approximate dates in Section 7.2 and adjust once dates are confirmed.

### 7.4 Holidays falling on a weekend

A public holiday that falls on an employee's weekend is **not carried over** to another day, unless the relevant government announces an alternative day, in which case the alternative day applies.

### 7.5 Working on a public holiday

- Employees are not expected to work on a public holiday of their Anchor Office.
- Work on a public holiday needs prior manager approval, for example for a Smart Machine outage at a retail location.
- Non-exempt employees are paid 200% or take TOIL, as set out in Section 6.1.
- Exempt employees who are asked to work a public holiday should receive a day off in lieu agreed with their manager.

### 7.6 Public holidays during leave

A public holiday that falls inside a period of annual leave is not deducted from the leave balance (see VND-HR-004).

## 8. Prayer and Religious Observance

Vendelligent respects the religious observance of all employees. Employees may take reasonable short breaks for prayer, which do not need to be recorded as leave and do not extend the working day beyond the standard hours. Quiet prayer space is available in the Riyadh and Dubai offices. Employees of any faith may ask their manager for reasonable adjustments to schedules for religious observance; if a manager cannot agree, they must escalate to People & Culture through PeopleDesk before refusing. Hajj leave is covered in VND-HR-004.

## 9. Field Operations Shifts and On-Call

- Field Operations technicians work scheduled shifts agreed with the Director of Hardware & Field Operations, set to meet the opening hours of retail locations hosting Smart Machines. Shifts must still total no more than 40 hours in a week before overtime applies.
- Shift rosters are published at least 7 days in advance in VendHub. Roster changes made with less than 48 hours' notice require the technician's agreement.
- Technicians on the on-call rota receive an **on-call allowance of SAR 400 per week**. Being on call means being reachable and able to reach the assigned region within the response time set by the Director.
- Hours spent actually responding to a call-out are working hours, and are paid as overtime at 150% (or 200% on weekends and public holidays, or TOIL) if pre-approved or, in an emergency, approved by the manager the next working day.
- Under the regional leave blackout rule, no more than 25% of a regional team may be on leave in the same week (see VND-HR-004).

## 10. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1. What are my core hours?**
Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 local time. You can start between 07:30 and 10:00 but must be available during core hours.

**Q2. I work in the Remote Engineering Hub. Which weekend and holidays do I follow?**
You follow your Anchor Office, shown in VendHub. For Riyadh the working week is Sunday to Thursday and KSA holidays apply; for Dubai it is Monday to Friday and UAE holidays apply.

**Q3. By when must I tell my manager I am sick?**
Before 10:00 on the first day of absence. A medical certificate is needed for more than 2 consecutive days.

**Q4. What happens if I miss work without telling anyone?**
Three unreported absences in a rolling 30 days trigger an attendance review.

**Q5. Do I get paid overtime as an engineer?**
No. Exempt roles do not receive overtime pay. Overtime at 150% (or 200% or TOIL on weekends and public holidays) applies to non-exempt roles such as Field Operations technicians, when pre-approved.

**Q6. How long is the working day during Ramadan?**
Six hours for all employees in the KSA and UAE offices.

## 12. Exceptions and Escalation

Exceptions to working hours (for example a permanent adjusted schedule for health or caring reasons) must be requested through PeopleDesk under the category "Leave & Attendance" and approved by the line manager and the Head of People & Culture. Disputes about overtime pay are P2 payroll cases in PeopleDesk (first response within 1 working day). Concerns about unfair treatment may be raised as a grievance under VND-HR-002.
