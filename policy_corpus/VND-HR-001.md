## 1. Purpose

This Code of Conduct sets the standards of behaviour expected of everyone who works for Vendelligent Technologies LLC ("Vendelligent" or "the Company"). It turns the value **Respect by default** and the Company's other values into specific, testable rules so that employees, consumers, retail partners and brand partners can trust the Company.

## 2. Scope

This Code applies to all employees (FT, PT), contractors (CT) and interns (IN), in Riyadh, Dubai, the Remote Engineering Hub, at retail partner sites where Smart Machines are installed, at Company events, on business travel, and online whenever conduct is connected to the Company. Failure to follow this Code may lead to action under VND-HR-011 Disciplinary Policy and Procedure.

## 3. Definitions

- **Conflict of interest**: a situation in which a personal, financial or family interest could influence, or appear to influence, a decision made for the Company.
- **Gift or hospitality**: anything of value (goods, meals, entertainment, travel, discounts) given to or received from a person outside the Company.
- **Public official**: a government employee, regulator, or employee of a state-owned entity, in any country.
- **Brand partner**: a perfume, coffee or other brand whose products are sold through Smart Machines or the Marketplace. **Retail partner**: the operator of a location hosting Smart Machines.
- **Consumer**: a member of the public who interacts with a Smart Machine or the Marketplace.
- **SpeakUp**: the Company's anonymous web reporting channel.

## 4. Standards of Behaviour

### 4.1 Core standards

Every person must:

1. Treat colleagues, consumers, partners and suppliers with courtesy and dignity.
2. Act honestly and accurately in records, timesheets, expense claims and reports.
3. Comply with the laws of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, the UAE and any country in which they work, and with Company policies.
4. Respect local customs and religious practice, including during Ramadan and in public retail environments.
5. Not engage in harassment, discrimination, bullying or retaliation (see VND-HR-002). These are zero-tolerance behaviours.
6. Raise concerns early and cooperate honestly with any review or investigation.

### 4.2 Zero-tolerance conduct

The following are treated as potential gross misconduct and may lead to dismissal without prior warnings: harassment, discrimination, retaliation, bribery, falsifying expenses, unauthorised disclosure of data, working under the influence of alcohol or substances, and violence or threats of violence.

## 5. Conflicts of Interest

### 5.1 Disclosure

Employees must disclose any actual or potential conflict of interest to their line manager and in a PeopleDesk ticket (category "Other" or "Policy Question") **within 5 working days** of becoming aware of it. Examples include:

- a close family member or partner working for, or owning, a brand partner, retail partner, supplier or competitor;
- a financial interest in a business that deals with the Company;
- outside employment or consulting in the retail, vending, AI or consumer-products field;
- hiring, managing or evaluating a relative or close personal partner.

### 5.2 Management

The Head of People & Culture decides, with Legal & Compliance where needed, whether to approve, restrict or prohibit the activity. The employee must not take part in any decision about the other party until the conflict has been cleared. Outside work is permitted only if it does not compete with the Company, use Company time, equipment or confidential information, or reduce performance.

## 6. Gifts and Hospitality

### 6.1 Rules

| Situation | Rule |
|---|---|
| Gift value up to SAR 150 | May be accepted or offered if infrequent, transparent and not intended to influence a decision. Registration not required. |
| Gift value above SAR 150 | Must be registered in PeopleDesk within 5 working days of receipt or offer. |
| Gift value above SAR 300 | Not permitted. Must be politely declined or returned. If refusal would cause offence, hand the item to People & Culture. |
| Cash or cash equivalents (gift cards, vouchers, transfers) | Never permitted, at any value. |
| Gifts to or from public officials | Not permitted without prior written approval from Legal & Compliance. |
| Gifts during a tender, contract renewal or pricing negotiation | Not permitted. |

The **gift cap is SAR 300** per gift. Repeated small gifts from the same source that together exceed SAR 300 in a calendar year are treated as one gift above the cap.

### 6.2 Hospitality and client entertainment

Business meals and events must be reasonable and have a business purpose. Client entertainment is limited to a maximum of **SAR 300 per head**, attendees must be listed in the expense claim, and Director approval is required if the total exceeds SAR 1,500 (see VND-HR-006). Alcohol is not reimbursable.

## 7. Anti-Bribery and Corruption

1. Employees must not offer, promise, give, request or accept a bribe or improper payment in any form, directly or through a third party.
2. Facilitation payments (small payments to speed up a routine government action) are not permitted.
3. Any approach for a bribe or suspicious payment request, especially in connection with retail partner site access, permits, customs or brand-partner contracts, must be reported to Legal & Compliance or through SpeakUp immediately.
4. Third parties acting for the Company (agents, installation subcontractors) must be told that this Code applies to them.

Bribery is zero tolerance and may also be a criminal offence.

## 8. Confidentiality

Employees must protect Confidential and Restricted information both during and after employment. This includes consumer data captured by Smart Machines (interaction logs, purchase history), payment data, brand-partner pricing, model weights and training data, source code, and employee records.

- Do not disclose this information outside the Company without written authorisation.
- Do not store it in personal email, personal cloud storage or public AI tools. See VND-HR-008.
- Do not discuss confidential matters in public places or on social media.
- Return or delete all Company information when employment ends (see VND-SOP-102).

Unauthorised disclosure of data is a zero-tolerance offence.

## 9. Social Media and Public Statements

1. Only the CEO and the people designated by the CEO may speak on the Company's behalf to media, regulators or the public.
2. Employees may not publish statements, images or videos that disclose Confidential or Restricted information, unreleased products, Smart Machine screens showing consumer interactions, or partner commercial terms.
3. Employees must not make false, defamatory or disparaging statements about the Company, colleagues, consumers, retail partners or brand partners.
4. On personal accounts, employees who identify themselves as Vendelligent staff must make clear that views are their own.
5. Photographs or videos of Smart Machines at retail partner sites may be shared only if they show no consumer, no partner staff and no confidential content, and have been cleared by Marketplace & Brand Partnerships if used commercially.
6. Posting about brand partners or retail partners is allowed only if accurate, respectful and consistent with this Code. Do not announce partnerships or launches before official publication.

Nothing in this section prevents a protected report through SpeakUp or to a competent authority.

## 10. Conduct on Retail Partner Sites (Field Operations)

Field Operations technicians and anyone visiting a retail partner site represent the Company in front of the public and partner staff.

| Area | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Dress | Company-issued uniform or smart, modest, clean clothing, Company ID visible at all times, PPE issued per site |
| Safety | Follow site rules and Site Safety training; use PPE; report incidents to the Director of Hardware & Field Operations and the site contact the same day |
| Access | Enter only authorised areas; follow site security and badge rules; do not tailgate |
| Behaviour | Courteous and professional with site staff and the public; no arguments in public areas |
| Work practices | Keep working areas tidy; minimise noise and disruption during trading hours; remove waste |
| Property | Do not take, use or move partner property or stock; do not accept gifts from site staff above the limits in section 6 |
| Machines | Do not open or service a Smart Machine in view of consumers in a way that exposes consumer data or payment components |

Visits to sites during prayer times and Ramadan hours should be planned to respect local practice and site hours.

## 11. Alcohol, Drugs and Substances

- Working under the influence of alcohol, illegal drugs or misused medication is not permitted and is zero tolerance.
- Alcohol and illegal drugs are not permitted on Company premises, in Company vehicles, at retail partner sites or at Company events, and are not reimbursable as an expense.
- Employees taking prescribed medication that could affect safety (for example for work at heights or driving a Company vehicle) should tell their manager or People & Culture in confidence.
- Employees who are struggling with substance use are encouraged to use the confidential Employee Assistance Programme (EAP), which provides 6 free counselling sessions per year.

## 12. Use of Company Resources

1. Company equipment, vehicles, accounts and systems are for business use. Incidental personal use is allowed if it is limited and does not break any policy.
2. Employees must protect assets registered in SecureVend (asset tag `VT-xxxxx`) and report loss or theft within 24 hours.
3. Okta MFA must not be shared or bypassed. Do not install unapproved software or use unapproved AI tools with Company data.
4. Company time must not be used for outside business activities.
5. Expenses must be accurate and supported by receipts. Falsifying an expense claim is zero tolerance.
6. Use of Company resources may be monitored in line with VND-HR-008 and applicable law.

## 13. Conduct Toward Consumers Interacting with Smart Machines

Consumers trust the Company with their preferences, voice and image. Employees must:

1. Treat every consumer respectfully, including when handling complaints or machine faults.
2. Never view, copy or share consumer interaction data (face or voice logs, purchase history) except for an approved business purpose with the required access.
3. Never use consumer data for personal purposes or share it outside the Company.
4. Never manipulate the AI assistant's recommendations to mislead a consumer, favour a brand for personal benefit or hide pricing.
5. Report suspected data exposure to SecureVend (`security` queue) and Slack #security-incidents within **1 hour** of discovery.
6. Assist consumers in difficulty (a machine that has taken payment but not dispensed, for example) and direct them to Customer Success.

## 14. Reporting Concerns

### 14.1 How to report

| Channel | Use for |
|---|---|
| Line manager | Any concern, where the employee is comfortable |
| PeopleDesk | "Grievance" or "Harassment/Discrimination" categories (confidential, visible only to People & Culture) or "Policy Question" |
| **SpeakUp** | Anonymous reporting of suspected misconduct, bribery, fraud, data misuse or retaliation |
| Legal & Compliance | Bribery, legal and regulatory concerns |

### 14.2 Protection

No one will suffer retaliation for a report made in good faith, even if it is not substantiated. Retaliation is itself zero tolerance. Knowingly false reports may lead to disciplinary action.

## 15. Consequences of Breach

Breaches are handled under VND-HR-011. The stages are: verbal warning, first written warning (valid 6 months), final written warning (valid 12 months) and dismissal. Gross misconduct, including every zero-tolerance behaviour in section 4.2, can skip stages. Employees have the right to be accompanied by a colleague at hearings and may appeal within 5 working days to a Director not previously involved. Contractors and interns may have their agreements ended in line with their terms.

### 15.1 Frequently asked questions

**A brand partner sent me a bottle of perfume worth SAR 220. What do I do?** The gift is below the SAR 300 cap but above SAR 150, so register it in PeopleDesk within 5 working days.

**Can I accept a SAR 400 hamper from a supplier?** No. Gifts above SAR 300 are not permitted. Decline or return it, or hand it to People & Culture if refusal would offend.

**Can I post a photo of a Smart Machine at a mall?** Only if it shows no consumers, no partner staff and no confidential content, and you are not announcing an unreleased product or partnership.

**My cousin owns a coffee supplier that bids to work with us. Must I tell anyone?** Yes. Disclose to your manager and via PeopleDesk within 5 working days, and do not take part in the decision.

**Can I report something anonymously?** Yes, through SpeakUp.

**Can I have a drink with a client at a business dinner?** Alcohol is not permitted at Company events and is not reimbursable. Follow local law and this Code.

## 16. Roles and Responsibilities

- **Employees**: know and follow this Code, complete Code of Conduct training on day 1, disclose conflicts and report concerns.
- **Line managers**: model the standards, handle concerns promptly and refer serious matters to People & Culture the same day.
- **People & Culture**: maintains this Code, keeps the gift register, runs investigations and applies VND-HR-011.
- **Legal & Compliance**: advises on bribery, public-official gifts and conflicts.
- **CEO**: approves this policy.

## 17. Exceptions and Escalation

No exceptions are permitted to the zero-tolerance rules. Other exceptions (for example to the gift rules for a public ceremonial gift) require written approval from the Head of People & Culture and Legal & Compliance, recorded in PeopleDesk. Concerns involving a Director are escalated to the CEO.
