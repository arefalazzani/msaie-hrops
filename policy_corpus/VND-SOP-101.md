## 1. Purpose

This SOP defines how Vendelligent Technologies LLC ("Vendelligent", "the Company") takes a new employee from a signed offer to day 90 of employment. It ensures every new hire has a contract, a working laptop and access, mandatory training, medical cover, a buddy and structured check-ins, so that they can contribute to our work on Smart Machines and the AI-driven marketplace quickly and safely.

## 2. Scope

This SOP applies to all full-time (FT) and part-time (PT) hires, and in modified form (items marked below) to fixed-term/contractor (CT) and intern (IN) engagements. It covers the period from offer acceptance to the day-90 close-out. Probation rules apply to FT and PT employees only; probation lasts 90 calendar days and may be extended once by up to 90 days (maximum 180 days in total).

### 5.2 Visa and work-permit steps (KSA and UAE hires)

The timelines below are indicative and depend on government processing. P&C keeps the new hire informed in PeopleDesk.

| Stage | Owner | Indicative timeline |
|---|---|---|
| Collect passport copy, photograph, degree attestation and medical fitness result | New hire | Within 5 working days of contract signature |
| File work-permit / entry-permit application | P&C | Within 3 working days of a complete document set |
| Government approval | Authority | 5 to 10 working days |
| Entry (if outside the country) and medical test | New hire | Within 14 days of approval |
| Residence visa / ID issuance | P&C | 7 to 14 working days after medical test |

The new hire must not start work in the country until the correct permit status is confirmed by P&C. If the permit is delayed, the start date is moved by written agreement and the provisioning ticket is updated in SecureVend. Remote Engineering Hub hires are not sponsored for visas in their country of residence.

### 5.4 Mandatory Day-1 trainings

All hires must complete **Security Awareness**, **Code of Conduct** and **Data Protection** on Day 1. Hardware & Field Operations hires must also complete **Site Safety** before visiting any retail location. Access to production systems is suspended until the three core trainings are complete.

### 5.5 30/60/90-day check-in template

The hiring manager records answers in VendHub after each check-in.

| Check-in | Questions |
|---|---|
| Day 30 | What has gone well so far? Do you have the tools and access you need? Is your role what you expected? Is your buddy helping? Which goals are unclear? |
| Day 60 | Which goals have you achieved? Where do you need coaching? How is collaboration across teams? What would improve onboarding for the next hire? |
| Day 90 | How confident are you in your role? What are your goals for the next quarter's OKRs? What development support do you want? Any concerns about workload or wellbeing? |

### 5.6 Probation review at day 80

The hiring manager and P&C hold the review on day 80 and record one of three outcomes in VendHub before day 90:

1. **Confirm** the employee as permanent; benefits that require probation completion start.
2. **Extend** probation once by up to 90 days (maximum 180 days in total), with written targets.
3. **Terminate** during probation, with 1 week of notice (see VND-SOP-102 Employee Offboarding SOP).

### 5.7 Leave, benefits and stipend rules for new hires

- Annual leave accrues from Day 1 at 1.83 days per month (22 working days per year) but cannot be taken during the first 60 days, except for emergencies approved by the Head of People & Culture.
- Medical insurance for FT employees starts on Day 1; spouse and up to 3 children are added after probation. PT employees are covered as employee only.
- The one-time home-office stipend of SAR 1,500 is available to FT employees only after probation and not to Field Operations technicians.

## 7. Failure and Exception Handling

| Situation | Action |
|---|---|
| Background check not clear or delayed beyond 5 working days | P&C informs the hiring manager; the start date is not confirmed until checks clear. |
| Provisioning ticket raised fewer than 5 working days before start | P&C marks the SecureVend ticket urgent and tells the hiring manager; the new hire may start with a loaner laptop and limited access. |
| Visa or permit delayed | Start date moved by written agreement; P&C updates the new hire weekly. |
| Mandatory training not completed on Day 1 | Production access remains suspended; the hiring manager sets a completion date no later than Day 5. |
| Buddy unavailable | The hiring manager assigns a replacement within 2 working days. |
| Check-in missed | The hiring manager reschedules within 5 working days and informs P&C. |

## 8. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1. Can I take annual leave in my first month?** Leave accrues from Day 1 but cannot be taken during the first 60 days, except for emergencies approved by the Head of People & Culture.

**Q2. When does my medical insurance start?** FT employees are covered from Day 1. Spouse and up to 3 children are added after probation.

**Q3. When can I claim the home-office stipend?** After probation only, for FT employees: SAR 1,500 one time. It is not available to Field Operations technicians.

**Q4. What if my laptop is not ready on Day 1?** Contact SecureVend and tell your hiring manager. IT must have it ready by the working day before Day 1 if the ticket was raised 5 working days ahead.

**Q5. What happens at the day-80 review?** The outcome is confirmation, a one-time extension of up to 90 days, or termination with 1 week of notice.

## 9. Roles and Responsibilities

- **Hiring manager:** assigns the buddy, prepares goals, leads Day-1 welcome, holds all check-ins and the day-80 review.
- **People & Culture:** runs checks, contracts, visas, the SecureVend ticket, orientation, benefits enrolment and records.
- **IT (SecureVend):** prepares equipment and accounts and supports Okta set-up.
- **Buddy:** supports informal questions for the first 60 days and shares team norms.
- **New hire:** completes documents and trainings, attends check-ins and raises concerns early.

## 10. Exceptions and Escalation

Exceptions to this SOP, including early leave use or changed start dates, are requested through PeopleDesk and approved by the Head of People & Culture. Unresolved issues are escalated to the COO. Safety concerns are P1 and follow VND-SOP-104.
