## 1. Purpose

This policy explains how Vendelligent Technologies LLC ("Vendelligent", "the Company") organises hybrid and remote work, when employees may work from another country ("Work from Anywhere" or WFA), and the security, tax, immigration and insurance rules that protect both the employee and the Company. Vendelligent builds AI-powered retail technology, including Smart Machines in retail locations and an AI-driven marketplace, and handles consumer data and brand-partner information. Flexibility is valued (**Move fast, with care**), but it must never weaken security or create legal exposure for employees.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to employees of the Riyadh HQ, the Dubai office and the Remote Engineering Hub who are eligible for hybrid or remote work. It applies to interns and contractors only to the extent their agreements allow. Remote work abroad is requested through VendHub and follows **VND-SOP-106 Work-from-Anywhere (Remote Abroad) Request SOP**.

## 3. Definitions

- **Hybrid work**: a combination of working at the Anchor Office and working remotely from the employee's home in the country of the Anchor Office.
- **Anchor Office**: the office (Riyadh or Dubai) whose working week, holidays and core hours the employee follows; shown in VendHub.
- **Anchor days**: Tuesday and Wednesday, when teams are expected to be in the office together.
- **Remote Engineering Hub**: fully remote roles approved at hire, working from approved countries.
- **Work from Anywhere (WFA)**: working temporarily from a country other than the employee's usual country of work.
- **Approved Country**: a country on the list in Section 7.3.
- **Restricted location**: a location where WFA is never approved (Section 7.4).
- **Restricted data**: the most sensitive data class under VND-HR-008, including consumer personal data captured by Smart Machines, payment data, brand-partner pricing, model weights and training data, and employee records.

## 4. Hybrid Work

### 4.1 Default model

The default working model is **hybrid with 3 office days per week**. Tuesday and Wednesday are anchor days, and the third office day is agreed with the manager. Employees log their office days in VendHub (see VND-HR-003). For employees whose Anchor Office is Riyadh, anchor days are Tuesday and Wednesday of the Sunday to Thursday week; for Dubai, Tuesday and Wednesday of the Monday to Friday week.

### 4.2 Fully remote roles

Fully remote roles exist **only in the Remote Engineering Hub** and are approved at hire. An employee who is not in the Hub cannot convert to fully remote unless the Head of People & Culture approves a transfer into the Hub and the Anchor Office is recorded in VendHub.

### 4.3 Additional remote days

A line manager may, at their discretion, approve additional remote days beyond the hybrid default on a temporary or regular basis, taking into account team needs, performance and collaboration. This discretion cannot reduce office days below 3 on a permanent basis without a record in VendHub, and it does not override anchor days for team events or critical launches.

### 4.4 Field Operations

Hardware & Field Operations technicians are **site-based** and are **not eligible** for hybrid, fully remote or WFA arrangements because they install and maintain Smart Machines at retail locations. Field Operations managers and administrative staff may be eligible for hybrid work if the Director of Hardware & Field Operations agrees.

## 5. Workspace and Ergonomics

- Employees working remotely must have a safe, quiet, adequately lit workspace with reliable internet.
- Employees working remotely must keep Company confidential information out of sight and earshot of others (see Section 8).
- Full-time employees who complete probation may claim the one-time **home-office stipend of SAR 1,500** for a desk, chair and monitor, as set out in **VND-HR-007** Equipment, IT Assets and Home Office Policy. Items above SAR 500 need an itemised receipt, and the chair sub-cap is SAR 800. The stipend is not available to Field Operations technicians, part-time employees, contractors or interns. Claims are submitted under **VND-HR-006** Business Travel and Expense Reimbursement Policy and VND-SOP-105.
- Employees should take regular breaks and report workplace injuries that happen during working hours to their manager and People & Culture.
- Company equipment is supplied through SecureVend; faults and replacements are requested there, not through PeopleDesk.

## 6. Working Hours While Remote

Remote and hybrid employees follow the same working week, core hours (10:00 to 15:00 local time), flexible start (07:30 to 10:00), public holidays and Ramadan hours as their Anchor Office (VND-HR-003). Managers assess remote employees on outcomes, not on visible presence.

## 7. Work from Anywhere (WFA)

### 7.1 Allowance and approval basics

- Eligible employees may work from another country for up to **20 working days per calendar year**.
- The country must be on the **Approved Countries** list.
- The request must be submitted through the **WFA form in VendHub at least 10 working days** before the first day of travel.
- The line manager approves requests within the allowance. Approval is not automatic and depends on business needs, team coverage and security.
- WFA days are working days spent working abroad; weekends and public holidays of the Anchor Office are not counted. Annual leave taken abroad is not WFA.

### 7.2 Longer or non-approved WFA

WFA is not generally granted if it is:

- beyond **20 working days** in a calendar year;
- for **more than 30 consecutive days** in a single trip; or
- in a country that is **not on the Approved Countries list**.

These exceptional requests require approval from the **Head of People & Culture, Legal & Compliance and Finance** (for tax). Employees must not assume approval and must not travel until the decision is given in writing in VendHub.

### 7.3 Approved Countries (2026)

Saudi Arabia, United Arab Emirates, Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar, Egypt, Jordan, Turkey, United Kingdom, Ireland, Germany, France, Spain, Portugal, Netherlands, Italy, Poland, Canada, United States, India, Pakistan, Malaysia, Singapore and South Africa.

The list is reviewed annually by People & Culture, Legal & Compliance and Finance, and changes are published in VendHub.

### 7.4 Restricted countries

WFA is **never approved** in:

- any country under comprehensive sanctions; or
- any country where the Company VPN or Google Workspace is blocked.

No Smart Machine production data may be accessed from a restricted location under any circumstances.

### 7.5 Right to be in the country and visas

The employee is responsible for holding a valid right to be in the destination country as a **visitor** for the whole period (for example a valid passport and visa-free entry or a tourist or visitor visa). **Vendelligent does not sponsor visas or work permits for WFA** and will not pay for visa costs. Employees must not work in a country where their visitor status does not allow remote work for a foreign employer. Employees remain responsible for ensuring they can return to their country of residence, including any UAE or KSA residency requirements.

### 7.6 Tax, social security and immigration risks explained plainly

Working in another country can, in some cases, create obligations the employee and the Company did not expect:

- **Tax**: a country may treat a person who works there for a period as a tax resident, or may claim that Vendelligent has a taxable presence there (a "permanent establishment"). This can mean income tax for the employee or corporate tax for the Company.
- **Social security**: the Company may have to pay, or the employee may have to pay, contributions in the country where the work is performed.
- **Immigration**: some countries treat any work done while on a visitor visa as unlawful, even remote work for a foreign employer.
- **Employment law**: local labour rules may apply in some circumstances.

The 20-day allowance, the Approved Countries list and the approval process exist to keep these risks low. Finance and Legal & Compliance may decline any request, or ask an employee to return, if risk changes (for example if the country imposes new rules). The Company does not give personal tax or immigration advice, and employees should obtain their own advice where concerned.

### 7.7 Core hours coverage

Employees working abroad must cover **core hours of 10:00 to 15:00 Anchor Office time**. If the time difference makes this impractical, the manager may decline the request or agree a different arrangement in writing. The employee must remain reachable on Slack and video during these hours.

## 8. Security Requirements While Remote

These requirements apply to all remote, hybrid and WFA work, and are supported by VND-HR-008 Information Security, Data Protection and AI Acceptable Use Policy.

- Use only a **Company-managed laptop**; personal devices must not be used for Company work except Okta-managed mobile apps under MDM.
- Sign in through **Okta SSO with MFA** and connect to the **VPN** to access internal systems.
- Do **not** use public Wi-Fi unless the VPN is connected.
- Set the **screen lock to 5 minutes or less**.
- Do **not store Restricted data** (including customer and consumer data) on personal devices, and do not print confidential data at home.
- Do not allow others, including family members, to use the Company laptop or view confidential screens.
- Do **not ship Company equipment internationally**. The employee carries the laptop personally as hand luggage and must not leave it unattended.
- Report lost or stolen devices or suspected breaches to the SecureVend `security` queue and Slack #security-incidents within **1 hour** of discovery.

## 9. Insurance Coverage Abroad

The Company medical insurance covers **emergency treatment abroad for up to 30 days per trip**. It does not cover routine or planned treatment abroad, and trips longer than 30 days need prior review by People & Culture. Employees are responsible for travel insurance for personal travel and for belongings. Equipment lost or damaged abroad is reported in SecureVend within 24 hours, with a police report for theft.

## 11. Breach of This Policy

Working from a non-approved country, exceeding the WFA allowance, working from a restricted location, or breaching the security requirements in Section 8 is a breach of this policy. Breaches are handled under **VND-HR-011 Disciplinary Policy and Procedure**. Serious or deliberate breaches, such as accessing Smart Machine production data from a restricted location or storing Restricted data on a personal device, may be treated as gross misconduct. The Company may require the employee to return immediately, may recover costs and may suspend remote access.

## 12. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1. How many days a week do I have to be in the office?**
Three office days per week, including the Tuesday and Wednesday anchor days.

**Q2. Can I work fully remotely?**
Only if you are in the Remote Engineering Hub, which is approved at hire. Field Operations technicians are not eligible for hybrid or remote work.

**Q3. How many days can I work from another country?**
Up to 20 working days per calendar year, in an Approved Country, with manager approval requested at least 10 working days in advance through the WFA form in VendHub.

**Q4. Can I work from a country that is not on the list?**
Not generally. It requires approval from the Head of People & Culture, Legal & Compliance and Finance, and is not generally granted. Restricted locations are never approved.

**Q5. Will the Company sponsor a visa for my WFA trip?**
No. You must have a valid right to be in the country as a visitor.

**Q6. Can I send my laptop to the destination?**
No. Equipment must not be shipped internationally; you carry the laptop yourself.

**Q7. Am I covered by medical insurance while abroad?**
Emergency treatment abroad is covered for up to 30 days per trip.

## 14. Exceptions and Escalation

Exceptions must be requested through PeopleDesk under the category "Remote Work & WFA" (priority P3: first response within 2 working days and resolution within 7 working days). Employees who need to travel urgently for a family emergency should contact their manager and People & Culture immediately. Requests that need Legal & Compliance and Finance review are escalated by People & Culture. Disagreements are handled through VND-HR-002.
