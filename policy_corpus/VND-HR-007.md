## 1. Purpose

This policy explains which equipment Vendelligent Technologies LLC ("Vendelligent", "the Company") provides, how it is requested, tracked, protected, replaced and returned, and how the home-office stipend interacts with Company-owned equipment. It supports the values **Own the outcome** and **Move fast, with care**.

## 2. Scope

This policy covers laptops, monitors, mobile devices, rugged tablets, toolkits, Company vehicles, personal protective equipment (PPE), accessories and software licences issued by the Company, as well as furniture bought with the home-office stipend. It applies to all employment types. Eligibility differs by type and is stated below. Information security rules for data on devices are in VND-HR-008.

## 3. Definitions

- **Asset**: any Company-owned item with an asset tag, recorded in SecureVend.
- **Asset tag**: a unique identifier in the form `VT-xxxxx` attached to each asset.
- **SecureVend**: the IT service desk used to request equipment, report faults and report security incidents (ticket IDs look like `SV-2026-00456`).
- **MDM**: mobile device management software that enforces security settings on mobile devices.
- **Okta-managed app**: an application accessed through Okta SSO with MFA.
- **Depreciated value**: the original purchase price reduced on a straight-line basis over the replacement cycle of the asset.
- **Home-office stipend**: the one-time SAR 1,500 stipend described in VND-HR-006.

## 6. Asset Tags and Register

- Every asset is registered in SecureVend with an asset tag `VT-xxxxx` before it is issued.
- Employees must not remove, cover or alter the tag.
- The assigned user is responsible for the asset until it is returned and signed back in.
- Assets may not be lent to another person, including family members or colleagues without a business need, nor transferred without a SecureVend ticket.
- IT may audit asset holdings at any time and employees must present assets on request.

## 7. Acceptable Use

- Company equipment is provided for business use. **Incidental personal use** is permitted if it is reasonable, does not affect work, does not breach law or Company policy, and does not create a cost or security risk.
- Employees must not install unlicensed software, disable security tools, or allow others to use their Company device.
- Illegal, offensive or discriminatory content must not be stored or accessed (see VND-HR-001 Code of Conduct and Workplace Behaviour).
- Consumer data from Smart Machines must remain on approved Company systems and must never be copied to local or removable storage (see VND-HR-008).

## 8. Care and Security

- Devices must be locked when unattended and must never be left in a car, at a client site or in a public place.
- Full-disk encryption and automatic updates must remain enabled. Operating system and security updates must be installed within 7 days of release.
- Employees must keep devices clean and free from damage and report faults promptly.
- Devices are carried in hand luggage when travelling and are never checked in with baggage.
- Equipment must not be shipped internationally. When an employee works abroad under VND-HR-005 and VND-SOP-106, the employee carries the laptop personally.

## 9. Replacement Cycles

| Asset | Replacement cycle |
|---|---|
| Laptop | 3 years from issue |
| Monitor | 4 years from issue |
| Mobile phone (Company-issued) | 3 years from issue |
| Rugged tablet | 3 years, or earlier if unserviceable |

Assets may be replaced earlier only when the IT lead confirms a fault that cannot be repaired. Retired assets are wiped and recycled or disposed of by IT. Employees must not sell or give away retired assets.

## 10. Damage, Loss and Theft

- **Report within 24 hours.** Any damage, loss or theft of an asset must be reported to SecureVend within **24 hours** of discovery. If the event may involve a data breach, the security incident rules in VND-HR-008 also apply, which require a report within **1 hour** to the SecureVend `security` queue and Slack #security-incidents.
- **Theft requires a police report.** The employee must file a police report and attach the reference to the SecureVend ticket.
- IT will remotely lock and wipe a lost or stolen device where possible.
- Accidental damage is repaired by the Company. Where loss or damage results from negligence, such as leaving a device unattended in a car, the Company may recover part or all of the depreciated value after an assessment by the Head of People & Culture and the Director, subject to applicable law.

## 11. Repairs

1. Raise a SecureVend ticket describing the fault, with the asset tag.
2. IT diagnoses the fault and either repairs it, issues a loan device or arranges vendor repair.
3. The employee must not attempt repairs or use third-party repair shops.
4. Faulty assets are returned to IT and the register updated.

Field Operations technicians whose rugged tablets fail on site must report the failure immediately to their regional lead and raise a SecureVend ticket so a replacement is dispatched.

## 12. Accessories and Peripherals

Standard accessories (charger, headset) are included. Additional peripherals such as keyboards, mice, webcams and docking stations are available through a SecureVend request with manager approval. Peripherals bought personally are not reimbursed except under the home-office stipend or an approved exception. Peripherals that connect to Company devices must be approved by IT.

## 13. Mobile Devices, MDM and Personal Devices

- Company-issued mobile phones are enrolled in MDM, which enforces a passcode, encryption and remote wipe.
- **Bring your own device (BYOD) is limited to Okta-managed mobile apps.** Personal phones may be used to access Company email, Slack and calendar only through Okta-managed apps with MDM enrolment.
- Personal devices must never be used to store, download or process consumer data, Restricted data or source code.
- If an employee does not accept MDM enrolment, they may not access Company email on a personal phone.

## 14. Field Operations Equipment

- Field Operations technicians receive a **rugged tablet**, a **toolkit**, access to a **Company vehicle** and **PPE issued per site**.
- Tools must be signed out and returned in a serviceable condition. Missing or worn tools are reported on a SecureVend ticket.
- Vehicles may be used only for business, must be driven lawfully by licensed drivers, and fines are the driver's responsibility.
- PPE required by a site must be worn on that site. Employees must not enter a site without the required PPE.
- Field Operations technicians are not eligible for the home-office stipend.

## 15. Home Office Furniture and Ownership

- Eligible Full-time employees (hybrid or remote, after probation) may claim the one-time **SAR 1,500** home-office stipend under VND-HR-006, with a chair sub-cap of **SAR 800**. Items over SAR 500 need an itemised receipt.
- Items bought with the stipend (for example a desk or chair) **belong to the employee**, including on leaving the Company.
- **Company-issued monitors and other equipment belong to the Company** and carry an asset tag, even when used at home.
- Part-time employees, Fixed-term/Contractor employees, Interns and Field Operations technicians are not eligible for the stipend.
- The Company is not responsible for the safety of personally owned furniture; employees should ensure a safe and ergonomic workspace.

## 16. Software Licensing

- Only licensed software approved through SecureVend may be installed on Company devices.
- Software purchases go through SecureVend procurement. A one-off purchase under SAR 200 may be expensed under VND-HR-006.
- Open-source and AI tools must be approved before use with Company data (see VND-HR-008).
- Licences are Company property and revert on departure.

## 17. Return of Equipment on Exit

- All assets must be returned **within 5 working days of the last working day**.
- People & Culture and IT confirm returns in SecureVend under VND-SOP-102 Employee Offboarding SOP.
- Assets not returned or returned damaged beyond fair wear are deducted from the final settlement at their **depreciated value**, subject to applicable law.
- Access is revoked at the end of the last day.

## 18. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1. How often is my laptop replaced?** Every 3 years. Monitors are replaced every 4 years. Request replacement through SecureVend.

**Q2. I left my laptop in a taxi. What must I do?** Report it to SecureVend within 24 hours, and within 1 hour to the SecureVend security queue and #security-incidents if data may be exposed. IT will lock and wipe it.

**Q3. Who owns the chair I bought with the stipend?** You do. The Company owns company-issued monitors and anything with a VT- tag.

**Q4. May I use my personal phone for work email?** Only via Okta-managed apps with MDM enrolment.

**Q5. Can I take my Company laptop abroad?** Yes, only if you carry it yourself and your work abroad is approved. Shipping equipment internationally is prohibited.

**Q6. I am leaving next week. When must I return my laptop?** Within 5 working days of your last day. Otherwise, its depreciated value is deducted from your final settlement.

## 19. Roles and Responsibilities

- **Employees**: protect assets, report damage, loss or theft within 24 hours, and return assets on exit.
- **Line managers**: approve requests and confirm business need.
- **IT / SecureVend team**: tag, issue, repair, track and recover assets; maintain MDM.
- **People & Culture**: raise provisioning tickets and confirm returns during offboarding.
- **Finance & Administration**: process deductions and stipend claims.

## 20. Exceptions and Escalation

Exceptions to standard issue or replacement cycles require approval from the Director and the IT lead. Disputes about deductions are raised through PeopleDesk and decided by the Head of People & Culture. Misuse of equipment is handled under VND-HR-011 Disciplinary Policy and Procedure.
