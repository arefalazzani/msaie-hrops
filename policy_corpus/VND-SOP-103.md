## 1. Purpose

This SOP sets out the step-by-step procedure for requesting, approving, amending and cancelling annual leave (PTO), and for reporting emergency and sick leave, at Vendelligent Technologies LLC ("Vendelligent", "the Company"). It operationalises VND-HR-004 Leave Policy so that leave is planned fairly across Smart Machine field teams, engineering launches and finance close periods.

## 2. Scope

This SOP applies to full-time (FT) and part-time (PT, pro-rata) employees in Riyadh HQ, the Dubai office and the Remote Engineering Hub. Interns receive 1 day of leave per month. Fixed-term/contractor engagements do not accrue leave. Special leave types (maternity, paternity, bereavement, marriage, Hajj, study, unpaid) follow VND-HR-004, and requests are also submitted in VendHub.

## 3. Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|

## 5. Procedure

### 5.1 Step-by-step procedure

| Step | Who | Action | System | Timing / SLA |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Employee | Check the balance in VendHub: Leave, then My Balance. | VendHub | Before every request |
| 2 | Employee | Check the team calendar and blackout periods. | VendHub, Google Workspace | Before every request |
| 3 | Employee | Submit the request in VendHub stating leave type, start and end dates and notes (cover arrangements). Requests by email or Slack are not valid. | VendHub | Meeting the notice rule in section 5.2 |
| 4 | VendHub | Validate: no negative balance; notice rule; maximum 15 consecutive working days; blackout flags. Invalid requests are rejected with a reason. | VendHub | Instant |
| 5 | Line manager | Approve or reject, with a reason for rejection. | VendHub | Within 2 working days |
| 6 | VendHub | If no action after 3 working days, auto-escalate to the manager's manager. | VendHub | After 3 working days |
| 7 | Director | For requests over 5 consecutive working days, give Director approval in addition to the manager. | VendHub | Within 2 working days of manager approval |
| 8 | CEO / COO | For more than 15 consecutive working days, give CEO or COO approval. | VendHub | Within 5 working days |
| 9 | VendHub | Confirm to the employee, update the team calendar and deduct the balance. | VendHub | On final approval |
| 10 | Employee | Set an out-of-office message and hand over urgent work before leave starts. | Google Workspace, Slack | Last working day before leave |

### 5.2 Decision table: request length, notice and approver

| Request length (working days) | Minimum notice | Approver |
|---|---|---|
| 1 to 2 days | At least 3 working days | Line manager |
| 3 to 5 days | At least 10 working days | Line manager |
| More than 5 consecutive days | At least 20 working days | Line manager and Director |
| More than 15 consecutive days | At least 20 working days | Line manager, Director and CEO or COO |

Notice is measured in working days of the employee's Anchor Office. A request must never take the balance below zero; VendHub rejects any request that exceeds the available balance.

### 5.3 Leave taken in the first 60 days and carry-over

Leave accrues from day 1 at approximately 1.83 days per month (2.5 days/month after 5 completed years) but cannot be taken during the first 60 days of employment, except for emergencies approved by the Head of People & Culture. A maximum of 5 days may be carried into the next year and must be used by 31 March or it is forfeited. Leave is not paid out in cash except at termination.

### 5.4 Public holidays inside a leave span

Public holidays of the employee's Anchor Office that fall inside a leave span are not counted as leave days. For example, Eid al-Fitr in KSA (approximately 4 working days) inside a two-week leave request reduces the days deducted by those holidays. Weekend days are also not deducted. Holidays on a weekend are not carried over unless the government announces an alternative day. P&C posts confirmed Islamic holiday dates in VendHub at least 48 hours before.

### 5.5 Cancellation and amendment

- The employee may cancel an approved request in VendHub up to 1 working day before the first day of leave. Cancelled days return to the balance.
- Partial cancellation is permitted (for example, shortening a 5-day request to 3 days) under the same deadline.
- Extending or moving dates is treated as a new request and must meet the notice rule.
- After the leave has started, changes need manager approval through PeopleDesk.

### 5.6 Emergency leave

For an emergency such as a family crisis, the employee must obtain verbal approval from their line manager (or the manager's manager if unavailable) and must submit the request in VendHub within 2 working days of the first day of absence. Emergency leave is deducted from the balance. In the first 60 days, emergency leave requires approval by the Head of People & Culture.

### 5.7 Sick leave logging

- Notify the line manager before 10:00 on the first day of sickness (call, then Slack message).
- Sick leave is up to 30 working days per year: the first 15 at 100% pay and the next 15 at 50% pay. It does not accrue and resets on 1 January.
- Upload a medical certificate in VendHub within 3 working days when the absence is more than 2 consecutive days.
- Sick leave is not deducted from annual leave.

### 5.8 What the AI HR Assistant can and cannot do

The AI HR Assistant is a Tier-0 channel. It can check the employee's balance summary, explain the rules in this SOP, and draft a request summary or a PeopleDesk ticket. It cannot submit, approve, amend or cancel leave and cannot change records. Sensitive matters (medical details, harassment or legal threats) are always routed to a human PeopleDesk ticket.

### 5.9 Worked example

Amira (Riyadh, L2) has a balance of 7.5 days. On Sunday she wants to take 3 working days of leave starting the following Sunday (next week).

1. Balance check: 7.5 days is enough for 3 days.
2. Length: 3 days falls in the 3 to 5 day band, which needs at least 10 working days' notice.
3. Notice: the leave starts in 5 working days, so the notice rule fails and VendHub rejects the request.
4. Outcome: Amira can (a) reduce the request to 1 to 2 days, which needs only 3 working days' notice, (b) move the dates at least 10 working days out, or (c) if urgent, contact her manager and raise a PeopleDesk exception ticket, which the Head of People & Culture may approve if it is an emergency.

## 6. Checklists

### 6.1 Requester checklist

- [ ] Balance checked in VendHub (Leave, then My Balance); request does not exceed it.
- [ ] Team calendar and blackout periods checked.
- [ ] Notice rule met for the request length.
- [ ] Type, dates and notes entered; cover arranged.
- [ ] Approval received before booking non-refundable travel.
- [ ] Out-of-office and handover set before leave.

### 6.2 Manager checklist

- [ ] Review the request within 2 working days.
- [ ] Check team coverage and the 25% Field Operations cap.
- [ ] Give a written reason for any rejection.
- [ ] Remember that auto-escalation occurs after 3 working days.
- [ ] Send requests over 5 consecutive days to the Director.

## 7. Failure and Exception Handling

| Situation | Action |
|---|---|
| Request rejected for insufficient balance | Reduce the days, or use unpaid leave (up to 30 days a year) approved by a Director and the Head of People & Culture. |
| Notice rule not met | Move the dates, shorten the request, or raise a PeopleDesk exception ticket. |
| Manager unavailable | VendHub auto-escalates after 3 working days. |
| Blackout conflict | Choose other dates; the Director may allow an exception. |
| VendHub unavailable | Raise a PeopleDesk ticket; do not use email or Slack as the formal request. |
| Over 15 consecutive days | Seek CEO or COO approval before booking. |

## 8. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1. Can I request leave by email?** No. Leave requests are valid only through VendHub. PeopleDesk is for questions and exceptions.

**Q2. Do public holidays count as leave days?** No. Public holidays of your Anchor Office inside a leave span are not deducted.

**Q3. Can the AI HR Assistant approve my leave?** No. It can explain rules and draft a summary or ticket, but it cannot submit or approve leave.

**Q4. When do I need a medical certificate?** For sick leave over 2 consecutive days; upload it within 3 working days.

**Q5. How late can I cancel approved leave?** Up to 1 working day before it begins.

**Q6. Can I carry leave over?** Up to 5 days, to be used by 31 March.

## 9. Roles and Responsibilities

- **Employee:** plans leave, submits valid requests, logs sick and emergency leave on time.
- **Line manager:** decides within 2 working days and protects team coverage.
- **Director:** approves requests over 5 consecutive days and blackout exceptions.
- **CEO or COO:** approves more than 15 consecutive working days.
- **People & Culture:** maintains VendHub rules, posts holiday dates and handles exceptions.

## 10. Exceptions and Escalation

Exceptions are requested through PeopleDesk and decided by the Head of People & Culture, with a Director for blackout conflicts. Unresolved disputes are escalated to the COO.
