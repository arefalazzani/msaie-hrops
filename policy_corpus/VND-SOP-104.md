## 1. Purpose

This Standard Operating Procedure defines how Vendelligent Technologies LLC ("Vendelligent", "the Company") receives, classifies, prioritises, assigns, escalates, documents and closes HR cases. It ensures that every employee request or concern, whether it is a simple policy question or a serious allegation, reaches the right People & Culture owner within a predictable time, is handled confidentially, and is resolved within the service levels stated in section 6.

## 2. Scope

This SOP applies to every HR case raised by employees of the Riyadh HQ, the Dubai office and the Remote Engineering Hub, through any of the channels in section 5. It covers cases handled in PeopleDesk, cases that originate with the AI HR Assistant, anonymous reports received through SpeakUp, and matters raised in person. It does not replace the substantive rules in VND-HR-002 (Anti-Harassment, Anti-Discrimination and Grievance Policy) or VND-HR-011 (Disciplinary Policy and Procedure); it governs how cases are received and routed. IT equipment, access and security incidents are handled in SecureVend, not PeopleDesk.

## 3. Definitions

- **PeopleDesk**: the HR ticketing and case system. Ticket IDs look like `PD-2026-00123`.
- **SecureVend**: the IT service desk for equipment, access and security incidents. Ticket IDs look like `SV-2026-00456`.
- **VendHub**: the people portal (HRIS) holding profiles, balances, payslips and the policy library.
- **AI HR Assistant**: the Tier-0 chatbot channel. It answers policy questions and prepares drafts but cannot approve, change records or close cases.
- **SpeakUp**: the anonymous whistleblowing web form, protected from retaliation.
- **Anchor Office**: the office (Riyadh or Dubai) whose calendar an employee follows, shown in VendHub.
- **People & Culture partner**: the People & Culture team member assigned to an Anchor Office.
- **Working day**: a day in the employee's Anchor Office working week (Sunday to Thursday for Riyadh, Monday to Friday for Dubai), excluding public holidays.
- **Business hours**: the hours within a working day on which People & Culture is staffed; "4 business hours" is counted only within those hours.
- **Auto-restricted case**: a case whose visibility is limited by the system to People & Culture only.
- **Requester**: the person who raises the case.

## 5. Intake Channels

| Channel | How to use it | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| PeopleDesk (web or Slack app) | Raise a ticket and choose a category | Primary channel. Ticket ID issued immediately |
| AI HR Assistant (Tier-0) | Ask a policy question in chat | May prepare a DRAFT ticket or DRAFT email that the employee must confirm before it is sent |
| SpeakUp | Anonymous web form | For whistleblowing and concerns the reporter does not wish to attribute. People & Culture creates the PeopleDesk case on the reporter's behalf |
| In person | Speak to a People & Culture partner or the Head of People & Culture | The People & Culture partner logs the case in PeopleDesk the same working day |

A case raised through any channel receives a PeopleDesk ticket ID. A verbal or Slack-only report is not considered logged until the ticket exists.

## 6. Categories, Priorities and SLAs

### 6.1 Categories

PeopleDesk uses these categories: Leave & Attendance; Payroll & Compensation; Benefits; Remote Work & WFA; Expenses; Equipment (redirect to SecureVend); Onboarding; Offboarding; Grievance; Harassment/Discrimination (auto-restricted); Policy Question; Letter Request (salary certificate, NOC, visa letter); Other.

### 6.2 Priority and SLA table

| Priority | Definition | First response | Resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | Safety or harassment allegation | 4 business hours | Investigation started within 2 working days |
| P2 | Payroll error, visa or legal deadline | 1 working day | 5 working days |
| P3 | Benefits, letters, WFA | 2 working days | 7 working days |
| P4 | General policy question | 2 working days | 10 working days |

Grievance cases are triaged as P2 for first response. They are acknowledged within 2 working days and the investigation outcome is issued within 15 working days (extendable to 30), as set in VND-HR-002; this outcome timeline overrides the P2 resolution time.

## 7. Triage Rules and Routing

### 7.1 Triage rules

1. Cases in the **Grievance** and **Harassment/Discrimination** categories are auto-restricted and visible only to People & Culture.
2. **Equipment** cases are redirected to SecureVend, and the requester is given the SecureVend ticket reference.
3. **Payroll errors** are always P2.
4. Any allegation of harassment, discrimination, retaliation, violence or a safety threat is P1 regardless of the category chosen by the requester.
5. A visa or legal deadline within 5 working days raises the case to P2.
6. If a case fits two categories, the more sensitive category governs.

## 9. Assignment and Escalation Matrix

Cases are assigned to the People & Culture partner for the requester's Anchor Office. The partner must declare any conflict of interest, and the Head of People & Culture then reassigns the case.

| Trigger | Escalate to | Timing |
|---|---|---|
| Any P1 case | Head of People & Culture | Immediately on triage |
| A Director is the subject of or involved in the case | CEO, in addition to the Head of People & Culture | Same working day |
| Safety threat | COO and local emergency services, in addition to the Head of People & Culture | Immediately; emergency services first where life is at risk |
| Head of People & Culture is conflicted | CEO | Same working day |
| SLA breach risk | Head of People & Culture | When 75% of the SLA has elapsed |

## 10. Case Lifecycle Statuses

| Status | Meaning | Exit condition |
|---|---|---|
| New | Case received, not reviewed | Triage completed |
| Triaged | Category, priority and owner set | First response sent |
| In Progress | Owner is working on the case | Resolution delivered or information needed |
| Awaiting Employee | Owner needs information from the requester | Requester replies |
| Resolved | Resolution delivered | Requester confirms or objection window ends |
| Closed | Case complete | Final |

A case in Awaiting Employee is closed automatically after 5 working days without a reply, with a reminder sent on working day 3. The requester may reopen a closed case by replying within 10 working days; after that a new case is required.

## 11. Documentation and Confidentiality

- Record **facts only**: dates, times, places, words used, witnesses, and documents provided. Do not record speculation, opinions about character or assumptions about motive.
- Quote the requester's description where possible and label any information that is second-hand.
- Do not record medical details beyond what is needed to process the case.
- Case content is shared on a need-to-know basis only. Managers receive only what they need to act.
- Restricted data, including employee records, must not be placed in Slack or personal email. Use PeopleDesk.
- Case records are retained according to VND-HR-008.

## 12. AI HR Assistant (Tier-0)

**The AI HR Assistant may:**
- answer policy questions with citations to the relevant document ID;
- look up the requester's own profile and balances;
- prepare a DRAFT ticket or DRAFT email for the employee to review and confirm.

**The AI HR Assistant must not:**
- approve any request, modify any record or close any case;
- give legal or medical advice;
- handle harassment, discrimination, medical or legal matters.

When a harassment, discrimination, medical or legal matter is detected, the Assistant stops and shows a short, non-judgemental message. It says that a People & Culture colleague will help, and that a P1 or P2 ticket has been prepared for the employee to confirm. It does not ask for details, assess the facts or express opinions. These tickets are routed to a human owner.

## 13. Sensitive-Case Handling

1. Treat the requester with respect (Respect by default) and do not assess credibility at intake.
2. Confirm that the case is auto-restricted and that no automatic notification reveals its content.
3. Tell the requester what happens next, who will see the case, and the expected timeline.
4. Remind the requester of EAP (6 free confidential sessions per year) and the protection against retaliation.
5. Do not inform the subject of the complaint until the Head of People & Culture directs it.
6. Where a safety threat exists, act first on safety (section 9), then document.

## 17. Frequently Asked Questions

**Who can see a harassment or grievance case?**
Only People & Culture. These categories are auto-restricted. Line managers cannot view them.

**Can I report something anonymously?**
Yes. Use SpeakUp. Retaliation against reporters is prohibited.

**Can the AI HR Assistant approve my leave?**
No. It cannot approve leave, change records or close cases. Leave is requested in VendHub.

**My laptop is broken. Do I raise a PeopleDesk ticket?**
No. Equipment cases are redirected to SecureVend.

**What happens if I do not reply to a question from People & Culture?**
After 5 working days in Awaiting Employee, the case is closed automatically. You can reopen it within 10 working days.

**How fast will I get a response?**
It depends on priority: P1 within 4 business hours, P2 within 1 working day, and P3 and P4 within 2 working days.

## 18. Roles and Responsibilities

- **Requester**: gives accurate information and responds to questions promptly.
- **People & Culture partner**: triages, assigns, handles cases, and maintains documentation standards.
- **Head of People & Culture**: owns this SOP, accountable for P1 cases and for SLA performance.
- **Directors and managers**: refer concerns raised to them to People & Culture the same working day and keep them confidential.
- **CEO**: accountable for cases involving a Director.
- **COO**: approves this SOP and is accountable for safety-threat response.
- **SecureVend team**: handles redirected Equipment cases.

## 19. Exceptions and Escalation

Exceptions to this SOP require written approval from the Head of People & Culture and are logged in the case. Where the Head of People & Culture is involved, the CEO decides. Disagreements about priority are resolved by the Head of People & Culture. Safety threats override every other rule in this SOP.
